About the job
NVIDIA is the pioneer of accelerated computing, a supercharged form of computing at the intersection of computer graphics, high-performance computing, and artificial intelligence. Our work is transforming industries from gaming and design to science and autonomous vehicles and profoundly impacting every aspect of modern life. We are looking for passionate, creative, and driven individuals to join us.

We are seeking a highly motivated intern to join our Xlogs team and contribute to the development of our critical web services and user-facing dashboards. You will work on a live, production-level application, building and refining features that directly impact our users and internal stakeholders. This role is a hands-on opportunity to develop a functional product, tackling challenges across the entire technology stack.

What You'll Be Doing

Feature Development: Designing, developing, and implementing new dashboard features and user interface improvements using React and TypeScript.
Responsive Design: Translating wireframes and prototypes from designers into high-quality, pixel-perfect, and responsive web components and layouts.
Code Quality & Maintenance: Identifying, troubleshooting, and resolving bug fixes, optimizing component performance, and maintaining code health across the application.
API Integration: Integrating with and consuming modern web APIs to fetch, display, and manipulate dynamic data within the user interface.
Prototyping: Rapidly building and iterating on functional prototypes to test new ideas and gather user feedback.
Collaboration: Working closely with product managers, UX designers, and senior engineers to understand requirements, participate in code reviews, and deliver high-impact solutions.

What We Need To See

Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.
Strong foundational knowledge of web development principles (HTML, CSS, JavaScript).
Proficiency in TypeScript and hands-on experience with a modern frontend framework (React is strongly preferred).
Understanding of (or strong interest in) modern web APIs (e.g., RESTful services) and asynchronous programming.
A curious and proactive mindset. We are seeking individuals who demonstrate initiative, write clean code, and assume responsibility for their project deliverables.
Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.

Ways To Stand Out From The Crowd

A demonstrated passion for building intuitive, fast, and user-friendly web applications.
Familiarity with the modern web development ecosystem (e.g., Git, npm/yarn, testing frameworks).
Experience with responsive design principles and CSS-in-JS or pre-processors (e.g., Sass).
Knowledge of backend development (e.g., Node.js, Express) or database systems (SQL or NoSQL).
Previous project or internship experience involving web development.

This is a unique opportunity to acquire practical experience across the full software development lifecycle—from concept to deployment. Interns will not only master React and TypeScript but will also acquire invaluable knowledge regarding building and maintaining scalable, high-impact web services. If you are an active learner motivated to contribute tangibly to a real-world product, we encourage you to apply.

NVIDIA is widely considered to be one of the technology world’s most desirable employers. We have some of the most brilliant and talented people in the world working for us. If you're creative and autonomous, we encourage you to apply. NVIDIA is committed to fostering a diverse work environment and is proud to be an equal opportunity employer.
