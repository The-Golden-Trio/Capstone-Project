Senior Enterprise Solution Architect - Data

About the job
Do you enjoy solving complex data transformation challenges for large enterprises?Are you passionate about designing modern cloud data platforms and helping customers unlock the value of their data?Do you thrive in client-facing architecture roles where you can influence strategy, technology, and business outcomes? You will be part of our Cloud and Application Services team, working with customers, architects, consultants, and cloud specialists to design and deliver modern data platforms and transformation solutions. As a Senior Enterprise Solution Architect – Data, you will lead the architecture and design of enterprise-scale data solutions for strategic customers. You will work closely with business and technical stakeholders to assess current environments, develop target-state architectures, and guide the delivery of modern cloud-based data platforms, with AWS as a primary focus.What You Will Be Doing
Lead data architecture engagements from assessment and strategy through to solution design and implementation guidance. 
Design modern cloud-based data platforms and migration approaches for enterprise customers.

Mentor delivery teams and provide architectural governance throughout project lifecycles.

Knowledge of data governance, security, integration, and data management practices.

Experience supporting consulting, advisory, or presales engagements.
