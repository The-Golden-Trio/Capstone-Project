Step 1: AI-1 interviews the expert

  • An LLM probes for incidents, good and poor responses, situation facts
  • One interview per expert; merged notes mark agreement and differences
  • Probes only inside a human-written topic guide (Flanagan, 1954; Whetzel et al., 2020)
  • A tracker lists the seed fields still missing
  • The expert confirms a summary
  • Every turn is stored verbatim; each seed field links to its source turn
Dẫn tới câu hỏi 1 cho cô ở slide 33 (phạm vi AI-1 và AI-2 trong ĐACN).

AI-1: evidence and limits

What studies show

In 766 AI-led interviews, 94.8–99.8% of questions were open-ended and 0.1–0.4% hallucinated (Chopra & Haaland). A chatbot asking follow-ups drew more specific answers than a form (Xiao et al., 2020).

Weaknesses, gap

AI interviewers missed follow-ups in 88% of failures (Wuttke et al., 2025). An LLM elicited at most 73.7% of requirements and invented a figure (Korn et al.). Hence the tracker and confirmation. No study found of expert critical-incident interviews, or in Vietnamese.

Chopra & Haaland là working paper (ghi năm bản sửa 2026). Wuttke chỉ so sánh nhỏ: 5 phỏng vấn do AI, 6 do người.

Step 2: AI-2 drafts the game seed

  • Helps the Content Admin build context, characters, situations, canon events
  • Drafts are written from the expert's own words
  • The expert sets every scoring key and rubric anchor; the model only drafts
  • Machine structure check, then expert approval, before any player sees it
  • Schema follows evidence-centered design (Mislevy & Haertel, 2006; Shute, 2011)
  • Checkpoint 1 of 4 for generated content
Trả lời "QA kịch bản sinh", chốt thứ nhất. Chi tiết schema của seed (danh sách fact, thang chấm) chờ game design; nói ở slide 32.

Why experts own keys and rubrics

Rubrics

Rubrics generated by LLMs differ from those human graders use, and LLM graders take shortcuts. Analytic rubrics that reflect human grading logic narrow the gap (Wu et al.).

Drafted content

When GPT-4 drafted 210 examination questions, specialists required revision of 15% and judged 0.5% false (Klang et al., 2023).

Hai dẫn chứng cho quy tắc "DE đặt mọi khoá chấm và mốc rubric, model chỉ soạn".

Step 3: AI-3 helps the expert test

  • A volunteer expert has about seven hours and cannot read every answer
  • AI-3 runs test plays (testers, simulated answers) through AI-4 and AI-5
  • It ranks cases: judges differ by over one level, or a rule broke
  • Plus a blind random sample of unflagged cases
  • The expert rules and may refine anchors; rubrics are versioned (Shankar et al., 2024)
  • Checkpoint 2 of 4: expert-scored plays also validate the judge
Giai đoạn này chưa có chuyên gia và người chơi thật: nhóm đóng vai cả hai, cô kiểm một mẫu. Kết quả cho thấy bộ máy chạy được, chưa phải độ khớp với chuyên gia.

Why a random sample, and real plays

Random sample

LLM confidence separates right from wrong grades only weakly (AUC 0.57–0.64; Ferrer et al.), so flagged cases alone can hide errors. If n audited cases show no error, the rate is at most about 3/n at 95% (Hanley & Lippman-Hand, 1983).

Real plays

Persona-prompted LLMs answered with near-uniform accuracy, unlike 2,359 real students (Balog & Bakken, 2026). Simulated profiles correlated r = 0.72 with students on multiple choice (Lu & Wang, 2024).

Hanley & Lippman-Hand: mới đọc đoạn mở đầu, điều kiện n > 30 lấy từ bài nhắc lại 1995; cần đọc bản gốc trước khi trích con số. Người chơi giả lập chỉ tìm được vấn đề tương đối.

Step 4: AI-4 narrates during play

  • The engine, not the model, controls the order of canon events
  • Canon content is shown verbatim; approved variants are logged
  • The model writes only connecting narration and follow-up questions
  • The whole seed is in the prompt; the rubric and keys are not
  • Run-time checks are rules, not a model call, so no added waiting
  • A different model family reviews the logs afterwards
Trả lời "QA kịch bản sinh", chốt thứ ba (luật lúc chạy) và thứ tư (rà log). Luật: con số và tên riêng phải có trong seed hoặc trong câu người chơi gõ; engine đếm follow-up. Không nêu con số ngoài về giới hạn của phép kiểm; nếu cô hỏi bắt được bao nhiêu lỗi, trả lời là nhóm sẽ đo bằng bộ thử của mình.

AI-4: why these choices

Authored plot

Holding authored structure outside free generation is the established approach (Riedl & Bulitko, 2013).

Rules on facts

Wrong numbers (184) and names (105) led 418 accuracy errors (Thomson & Reiter, 2020).

Whole seed in prompt

When the source fits, long-context prompting beat retrieval on average (Li et al., 2024).

Thomson & Reiter là lý do luật nhắm vào con số và tên riêng.

Testing the run-time checks

  • A test set of scripted player turns in four classes
  • On-script, off-script, adversarial, rubric-fishing
  • We report the violation rate per class
  • Not yet built
Checkpoint 3 of 4 (luật lúc chạy) và 4 of 4 (rà log sau). Nói rõ bộ thử chưa dựng.

Step 5: AI-5 scores the playthrough

  • A separate judge: the scoring model is not the narrating model (DC-01)
  • No reference answer; each rubric criterion is scored by level descriptions
  • One call per situation per judge, at most three criteria
  • The call runs in the background; results show on the summary screen
  • Per criterion: a verbatim quote, then the rationale, then the level
  • The quote must be a substring of what the player typed
Thang chấm ghi là dự kiến; bảng chấm năng lực và bộ tiêu chí chờ game design (slide 32).

Why per situation, why a panel

Per situation

Batching several rubric checks into one call trades accuracy for cost, with large loss when evidence is spread over a long trajectory (Peng et al., 2026). A verdict shifts with the other rubrics judged alongside it (Yu et al.).

A panel

A five-judge vote matched the best single judge (r = 0.627 vs 0.623; Kim et al., 2025). Judges also vary across runs (Haldar & Hockenmaier, 2025). We do not claim a panel is more accurate.

Lý do nêu cho panel là độ bền (đạt mức judge đơn tốt nhất mà không cần biết trước judge nào tốt nhất), không phải độ chính xác hơn.
Must the judge be stronger than the generator?

Not relative to the generator. The judge scores text a person wrote, so the two do not compete. It needs four other things (next slide). Each judge family is tested at two tiers in week 42; we choose the cheapest tier that is not clearly worse.

Trả lời câu hỏi "model chấm có cần mạnh hơn model sinh không".

What the judge does need

Knowledge

With no reference answer, judges matched experts only on questions they could answer (Krumdick et al.).

Capacity

ICC under 0.20 for small models, 0.41–0.57 for larger; 0.45 between two humans (Jung et al., 2025).

Reasoning optional

Two reasoning models were less valid than a smaller standard model (Yoshida, 2025).

Another family

Evaluators favour their own output and similar mistakes (Panickssery et al., 2024; Goel et al., 2025).

JobQuest không có đáp án mẫu, nên judge phải hiểu công việc IT và rubric phải mang tri thức miền. Ngoài ra Tan et al. (2025): khả năng làm judge đi theo khả năng giải bài.

Is an LLM judge reliable?

  • Conditionally, not by default
  • Agreement nears human level with a strong model, an explicit rubric, scored examples
  • This phase relies on the first two; scored examples come after expert rulings
  • Weakest on partially correct answers (Schleifer et al., 2026)
  • Evidence is English, essays, 2023–2024 models; none on Vietnamese role-play

Benchmark: no public one fits

  • Nothing public matches: ordinal rubric, short human answers, Vietnamese, IT work
  • References only: JudgeBench, BiGGen Bench, SEA-HELM
  • Ours: expert-scored playthrough transcripts against judge scores, per situation
  • For now the team stands in for experts; the advisor checks a sample
  • So results show the machinery works, not agreement with experts
  • The measures work on any ordinal scale, so they do not wait for game design

Framework for the LLM judge

  • Offline tests: Inspect AI, open source, UK AI Security Institute
  • Repeated runs, several graders, median reducer, ordinal Krippendorff's alpha
  • Our rubric needs a custom scorer
  • In the product: our own TypeScript module with schema-validated output
  • Prompts and rubrics live in data files shared by both
  • Not chosen: OpenAI Evals platform, deprecation announced 3 June 2026
Các công cụ này không có bài báo bình duyệt. Khoa học nằm ở phương pháp: chấm theo rubric có lập luận trước điểm (Liu et al., 2023) và panel judge (Verga et al.).

Is JobQuest AI-native?

Definition adopted

AI is constitutive of the core loop: remove it and the central activity collapses (Xu et al., preprint, 53 AI-native games). AI is part of design, operation and maintenance (Ericsson, white paper). No standards body defines the term.

Applied to JobQuest

AI works at all five steps. Without it the system can neither fit narration to a free-text answer nor score it. There is no substitute path: no keyword scoring, no scripted replacement.

Nêu thẳng hai nguồn neo của định nghĩa là một preprint và một tài liệu của hãng: đây là câu hỏi 4 cho cô. ITU-R M.2160 dùng thuật ngữ mà không định nghĩa. Không bàn tới prototype. Xu et al., Ericsson, ITU còn 🔍: đọc bản gốc trước khi dựng.

When the model provider fails

  • Generation and scoring, the core, stop
  • The session pauses; answers and progress are saved (NFR-04)
  • Play resumes when the AI returns
  • Sign-in, occupation map and profile stay available
  • Another provider is still AI, not a fallback (DC-02)
Trả lời nửa sau câu hỏi: khi AI sập thì còn chạy không. Không có đường thay thế như chấm theo từ khoá.

Comparing AI models

Four families

One for generation, three for the judge panel. Judges differ from the generator (DC-01) and from each other; bias toward related models falls as the relationship grows distant (Li et al., 2026).

Six criteria

Vietnamese capability, knowledge of IT work, response time, price, schema-constrained output, data terms.
