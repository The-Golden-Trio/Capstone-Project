### Cited Findings

**Persona 5 / Persona 5 Royal**
- Each of the five stats "can be a level between the numbers of 1 and 5". Rank names, low to high: Knowledge: Oblivious, Learned, Scholarly, Encyclopedic, Erudite. Charm: Existent, Head-turning, Suave, Charismatic, Debonair. Kindness: Inoffensive, Considerate, Empathetic, Selfless, Angelic. Guts: Milquetoast, Bold, Staunch, Dauntless, Lionhearted. Proficiency: Bumbling, Decent, Skilled, Masterful, Transcendent. — [RPG Site, "Persona 5 Royal Stats guide"](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/13328-persona-5-royal-stats-guide-increase-knowledge-guts-proficiency-kindness-and-charm-fast) [read page]
- "the EXP numbers are hidden away and not surfaced to the player." The player sees the rank word, not the points. — [RPG Site, same guide](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/13328-persona-5-royal-stats-guide-increase-knowledge-guts-proficiency-kindness-and-charm-fast) [read page]
- Thresholds differ by stat (rank 5 needs 192 points in Knowledge, 132 in Charm, 136 in Kindness, 113 in Guts, 87 in Proficiency). "Numerous activities increase the player's social stats. Each activity affects a particular social stat"; stats "can additionally increase when increasing social links with confidants." — [Samurai Gamers, "Social Stats Guide"](https://samurai-gamers.com/persona-5/human-parameters-maximize) [read page; guide site, thresholds are data-mined values not shown in game]
- A reader comment on that page says the musical notes shown on screen after an activity "are not the actual number of points that you get". — [Samurai Gamers, same page, comment section](https://samurai-gamers.com/persona-5/human-parameters-maximize) [snippet-grade: user comment]
- Ranks gate content (round 1): the Hanged Man Confidant needs Guts rank 4, the Star Confidant needs Charm rank 3. — [Player.One guide](https://www.player.one/persona-5-social-stats-guide-level-guts-charm-knowledge-kindness-proficiency-593545) [read page, round 1]

**Fallout 3 (G.O.A.T.)**
- Answers decide "which three skills get a little boost, called 'tags'"; "After the test, you can chat with Brotch to change those tags if you're not happy with them." — [Ludo.guide, "Future Imperfect"](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/fallout-3/fallout-3-walkthrough/prologue-vault-101/future-imperfect) [read page, round 1]
- "The GOAT consists of ten multiple choice questions, which will determine the best job for you in the Vault"; the player can "complain to Mr. Brotch and skip the test"; tags can be edited on handing the test in. — [GameBanshee, Fallout 3 walkthrough "Growing Up"](https://www.gamebanshee.com/fallout3/walkthrough/growingup.php) [snippet; page 403]
- Nine of the ten questions carry a skill; 13 job outcomes. — [GameSkinny, G.O.A.T. guide](https://www.gameskinny.com/?p=179921) [snippet; page 403]

**Pokémon Mystery Dungeon**
- Explorers of Time / Darkness: Serebii lists 64 questions; each answer gives 2 or 4 points to one or two of 16 natures (Docile, Timid, Jolly, Relaxed, Bold, Lonely, Sassy, Calm, Quirky, Hasty, Quiet, Rash, Impish, Hardy, Brave, Naïve); the result "is determined by what nature has the most points at the end"; "Once answered, it'll ask for your gender and voila, your Pokémon is decided." There is no reject button: "You never get the same set of questions so you can always restart if you do not get the Pokémon that you wish." — [Serebii, "Nature Test" (Explorers of Time & Darkness)](https://serebii.net/dungeon2/naturetest.shtml) [read page; fan-run reference site]
- Rescue Team DX (2020): "you'll be asked nine questions" (eight random, the ninth asks boy or girl); "whichever nature you have the most of combined with whether you chose to play as a boy or a girl" gives one of 16 Pokémon; "Remember that you aren't stuck with the Pokémon the game assigns you." The article counts 13 natures. — [iMore, "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX: all personality test answers"](https://www.imore.com/pokemon-mystery-dungeon-rescue-team-dx-all-personality-test-answers) [read page]
- Explorers of Sky picks 8 questions at random and "the Natures are not represented evenly". — [Bulbapedia, Explorers of Sky walkthrough, Chapter 0](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Appendix:Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky_walkthrough/Chapter_0) [snippet; fan wiki, page 403]

**Ultima IV**
- Question text as shown in play: "Thou art sworn to uphold a Lord who participates in the forbidden torture of prisoners."; "In thy youth thou pledged to marry thy sweetheart. Now thou art on a sacred quest in distant lands." The text never names the two virtues; the transcript's author adds the virtue names as labels. There are 28 possible questions (every pairing of eight virtues), and each answer eliminates the virtue not chosen. — [Let's Play Archive, "Ultima 4, 5 and 6", Update 1](https://lparchive.org/Ultima-4-5-and-6/Update%201/) [read page; player transcript]
- Seven are asked per game; the surviving virtue sets class, starting town, equipment and which companion cannot join. — [Ultima Codex, "Character creation"](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Character_creation) [read page, round 1; fan wiki]

**Final Fantasy Tactics**
- Compatibility "affects the amount of damage or healing and the chance for percentage-based actions, such as Talk Skill, Haste, Raise, and Lich, to work." Multipliers: Best ×1.5, Good ×1.25, Bad ×0.75, Worst ×0.5. Best needs the opposite sign and opposite sex; Worst is the opposite sign with the same sex; monsters count as sexless, so a Best or Worst pairing with a monster becomes Bad. The sign is read from the unit's status screen; Ramza's comes from the birthday the player enters (the guide's date-range section is marked incomplete). — [Andrea Robinson, "Final Fantasy Tactics Zodiac Compatibility Guide" v0.9, updated 30 Apr 2003, RPGamer archive](https://archive.rpgamer.com/games/ff/fft/info/fft_zodiac.txt) [read page; player-written guide hosted by RPGamer, not an official manual]
- The 2025 remaster's own guide describes the same effect in words (more damage, more healing, "better accuracy"; negative affinity the reverse). — [RPG Site, Ivalice Chronicles zodiac guide](https://www.rpgsite.net/guide/18604-final-fantasy-tactics-the-ivalice-chronicles-zodiac-compatibility-guide-chart-signs-list) [read page, round 1]
- Version 1.5.0 of The Ivalice Chronicles (article dated 17 June 2026) lists under new features: "You may now check a unit's zodiac sign and compatibility from its status screen." — [RPG Site news, "Square Enix updates Final Fantasy Tactics – The Ivalice Chronicles…"](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20719-square-enix-updates-final-fantasy-tactics-the-ivalice-chronicles-with-new-game-mode-zodiac-compatibility-function-more) [read page]

**The Elder Scrolls**
- Todd Howard on Oblivion's up-front class choice, relayed in Spanish from a Game Informer podcast: players would say "dios mío, llevo tres horas jugadas y me he equivocado de profesión" ("my god, I've played three hours and I picked the wrong profession"); the team asked "¿qué hubiera pasado si nadie nos hubiera hecho esa pregunta en el juego?" ("what if nobody had asked us that question in the game?"); skills that rise through use are "un sistema muy, muy elegante, que se equilibra a sí mismo". — [3DJuegos, 7 Feb 2011](https://www.3djuegos.com/juegos/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim/noticias/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim-contara-con-un-sistema-de-clases-110207-15741/amp) [read page; Spanish-language relay, not the podcast itself]
- A hands-on preview contrasts earlier games, "where you have to navigate through options such as your character's birthsign and skills immediately", with Skyrim, where "your main task … is to choose your race, gender, and appearance." No designer is quoted on the reason. — [Game Informer, Jeff Cork, "A New Beginning", 17 Aug 2011](https://gameinformer.com/games/the_elder_scrolls_v_skyrim/b/ps3/archive/2011/08/17/gamescom-skyrim-preview.aspx) [read page]

**The Sims**
- The Sims 2 Create-a-Sim: the displayed zodiac sign follows the personality points; "You can usually move around a couple points before the zodiac sign changes." — [Pleasant Sims, "Sims 2 Custom Neighborhood Tutorial (Part 4 – Residents)"](https://pleasantsims.com/sims-2-custom-neighborhood-residents/) [read page; fan blog]
- 25 points over five traits (Neat, Outgoing, Active, Playful, Nice) and twelve preset distributions, one per sign. — [StrategyWiki, "The Sims 2 / Create-a-Family"](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/The_Sims_2/Create-a-Family) [snippet; page 403]
- The Sims 4: traits can be reopened with the `cas.fulleditmode` cheat or a potion bought with satisfaction points. — [Gfinity, "Sims 4: How To Change Traits With and Without Cheating"](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/sims-4-how-to-change-traits-with-without-cheats) [snippet]

**Disco Elysium**
- Mechanics (round 1): 24 skills, each able to "speak directly to the player to influence their decisions"; Thought Cabinet thoughts take in-game hours to internalise and can bring "negative effects"; a thought can be dropped at the cost of a skill point. — [Wikipedia, "Disco Elysium"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco_Elysium) [read page, round 1]
- Lead designer Robert Kurvitz in a GameSpot "Audio Logs" interview, as summarised by a forum post: the dialogue interface was modelled on Twitter, the writing is "aggressive and personal" to hold attention, with "critical information repeated multiple times by the protagonist's various skills during key moments"; for a next version he wants "thoughts have an impact on their neighbors in adjacent slots in the cabinet." — [RPG Codex, "Robert Kurvitz talks about writing Disco Elysium on GameSpot Audio Logs", 14 Jan 2020](https://rpgcodex.net/article.php?id=11309) [read page; forum relay of a video interview]
- The studio's dev blog said the cabinet would "represent the game's perk, trait, class and reputation systems all at once"; GameSpot's headline calls it "the feature that almost sunk Disco Elysium". — [GameBanshee relay of ZA/UM dev blog](https://gamebanshee.com/39hc9); [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/how-the-feature-that-almost-sunk-disco-elysium-was/1100-6472676/) [both snippet]

**Pottermore / Wizarding World**
- The official help centre has an article titled "Does the Wizarding World Sorting Ceremony feature the original questions written by J.K. Rowling?"; the search excerpt says yes. — [Wizarding World help centre](https://help.harrypotter.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034696911-Does-the-Wizarding-World-Sorting-Ceremony-feature-the-original-questions-written-by-J-K-Rowling) [snippet; page 403]
- Structure (round 1): eight questions drawn from 28, first and last from fixed sets of three; figures come from a fan's reverse-engineering. — [Bookstacked](https://bookstacked.com/popular/jk-rowling/harry-potter/sorting-hat-quiz-pottermore-answers-questions-cheats/) [read page, round 1; relays a Reddit analysis]

**Silent Hill: Shattered Memories**
- Writer and designer Sam Barlow: the system takes "a lot of data about your personality"; "we're not assessing what you, as Harry, do, but what you as a player do"; "Every 'change' in the game" is "based on polling your personality, the sum total of all things you have done up to that point"; the profile changes dialogue, character appearance, locations, answering-machine messages, lighting and ambient audio. He wanted to "upset the player's expectations and make them feel uncomfortable", and presents the player "not knowing exactly what's happening under the hood" as part of the appeal. The technology "is based on a lot of existing research"; no personality model is named. — [The Telegraph interview with Sam Barlow, 5 Feb 2010, as archived by Silent Hill Memories](https://www.silenthillmemories.net/creators/interviews/2010.02.05_barlow_telegraph_en.htm) [read page; fan archive of a press interview]
- A debug build exposes "the seven main values that attribute to the psychological profile". — [The Cutting Room Floor, "Development: Silent Hill: Shattered Memories"](https://tcrf.net/Development:Silent_Hill:_Shattered_Memories) [snippet; fan wiki]

**Until Dawn**
- "an intermission between each chapter in which psychiatrist Dr. Alan Hill addresses the player directly"; he analyses "the player's fears along with choices they have made". Reviewer Dean Carter praised "the intermission sessions that became increasingly disturbing as the game progressed." — [Wikipedia, "Until Dawn"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Until_Dawn) [read page, first 100,000 characters]
- Co-writer Graham Reznick: "The analyst stands in for the video game designers and the writers as we are creating the game." — search excerpt returned alongside [Wikipedia, "Until Dawn"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Until_Dawn) [snippet; originating page not identified, and the sentence was not in the portion of the Wikipedia page read]
- Fears named in sessions return as props and change episode titles; story impact is small (round 1). — [University of Konstanz GameLab wiki](https://wiki.uni-konstanz.de/gamelab/index.php/Until_Dawn) [read page, round 1; student wiki]

### Inferences
- The games with the longest-lived reputations for "it knew me" (Ultima IV, Shattered Memories) hide what is measured, but they are single-player fiction for adults. JobQuest's users include minors and the product stores the data, so the transferable part is the *question form* (a concrete scene, no trait word in it), not the secrecy.
- Persona 5 turns a number into a word and hides the number. That is a design for motivation, not for self-knowledge: the lowest rank words ("Bumbling", "Oblivious", "Milquetoast") are mild insults the player is meant to climb away from.
- Where the quiz result has mechanical weight, the later entries let the player overrule it (Fallout 3 in 2008, Rescue Team DX in 2020). The earlier Explorers games offered only a restart, and the reference site presents restarting as the way to get the Pokémon you want. Pottermore, whose result is a label with no mechanics, is the exception with no override found.
- Final Fantasy Tactics is the clearest negative case for the constellation layer: a birthday the player types in for flavour can halve damage or add half to it, and the 2025 remaster gained an in-game compatibility display only in its 1.5.0 patch (article dated June 2026). Whether the 1997 original showed compatibility anywhere in its interface was not checked.

### Gaps
- **Persona 5 pentagon**: megatenwiki.com (403), the Fandom mirror (bot wall) and two guides gave no description of the stat screen. A team member can settle this in two minutes with a screenshot of the in-game Stats menu; until then the report should not state the shape.
- **Persona 3 and 4**: nothing opened; no comparison is offered.
- **Fallout: New Vegas**: test order and whether results bind are still round-1 snippets (GamerGuides, GameBanshee/Avellone).
- **Original Rescue Team (2005) and Super Mystery Dungeon**: question counts and reject rules not read from an opened page.
- **Dragon Quest III question count**, original 1988 release versus each remake: not resolved.
- **Final Fantasy Tactics reception**: no review or player survey opened that calls the hidden mechanic good or bad. One search excerpt of an unidentified guide says veterans had relied on "external knowledge"; not citable.
- **Bethesda on birthsigns**: no designer statement found; the Howard remark is about classes and reaches us through a Spanish news relay.
- **The Sims (2000)** zodiac rule and **The Sims 4** trait changes: not read from an opened page.
- **Pottermore**: no statement from Rowling or the studio on why the questions are oblique.
- A search excerpt claims the Shattered Memories team "started with the five-factor personality model" and dropped a "psychopathy" factor. It comes from a blog repost of an interview on an unrelated domain and does not match the five-factor model as usually described; do not cite.

## 2. Developer intent and lessons

### Takeaway
Only three first-hand designer positions were found, and they point the same way: do not make players commit before they have played (Howard), measure what the player does, not what they say (Barlow), and have the system say important things back to the player more than once (Kurvitz, relayed). No talk or post-mortem on quiz-based character creation, question count, or revealing what is measured was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Commitment before play causes restarts.** Howard's account of Oblivion players discovering after three hours that they had chosen wrongly, and the team's question "what if nobody had asked us that question", led to skills that grow by use. — [3DJuegos, 7 Feb 2011](https://www.3djuegos.com/juegos/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim/noticias/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim-contara-con-un-sistema-de-clases-110207-15741/amp) [read page; relay]
- **Behaviour over self-report.** Barlow: "we're not assessing what you, as Harry, do, but what you as a player do"; changes come from "the sum total of all things you have done up to that point". — [Telegraph interview, 5 Feb 2010](https://www.silenthillmemories.net/creators/interviews/2010.02.05_barlow_telegraph_en.htm) [read page; fan archive]
- **Opacity was a deliberate horror device.** Barlow wanted players to "feel uncomfortable" and not know "exactly what's happening under the hood". — [same interview](https://www.silenthillmemories.net/creators/interviews/2010.02.05_barlow_telegraph_en.htm) [read page; fan archive]
- **Repeat what matters.** Disco Elysium has "critical information repeated multiple times by the protagonist's various skills during key moments". — [RPG Codex relay, 14 Jan 2020](https://rpgcodex.net/article.php?id=11309) [read page; forum relay]
- **First minutes.** Celia Hodent (Epic Games), GDC 2016, "The Gamer's Brain, Part 2: UX of Onboarding and Player Engagement": "Engaging your audience within the first minutes of play is a delicate endeavor, and has become a critical aspect of development in the era of free-to-play games." The page lists pitfalls, UX guidelines and Fortnite examples but no specific numbers. — [GDC Vault session page](https://gdcvault.com/play/1022951/The-Gamer-s-Brain-Part) [description only]
- **Question counts observed in shipped games** (not a designer statement): Ultima IV 7; Pottermore 8; Rescue Team DX 8 plus gender; Morrowind 10; Fallout 3 10 (snippet). — sources as in section 1

### Inferences
- Shipped quizzes cluster at 7 to 10 questions. JobQuest's main flow asks 3 typed answers and 18 cards, about double the upper end, so the 18 cards need to feel like one fast gesture repeated, with visible progress, not like 18 questions.
- Howard's lesson applies to the stage tap, the interest cards and the constellation choice alike: anything set in the first five minutes should be editable from the profile page without a penalty.
- Barlow's secrecy should not be copied. The same interview shows the alternative JobQuest already chose: say plainly that play is being read, and show the reading.

### Gaps
- No GDC talk, post-mortem or interview was found in which a designer discusses how many questions to ask, whether to reveal the scoring, or the effect of letting players override a quiz result.
- Hodent's talk content was not watched; only the session description was read.
- Howard's podcast and Kurvitz's interview were not heard first-hand.

## 3. Other mechanics: character from play, end-of-chapter summaries, identity pages

### Takeaway
Only two items here reached "read page": Oblivion's class proposal after the tutorial (round 1) and Detroit: Become Human's flowchart, which reviewers split on. Telltale's choice percentages, Detroit's world percentages and Fable's alignment were not verified and should not be cited as fact.

### Cited Findings
- Oblivion: the class is proposed in conversation after the tutorial, and "If you go through the tutorial without gaining any experience points, [Baurus] will default to assume you are an Acrobat"; the player may accept, pick another of 21, or build one. — [UESP, "Oblivion:Classes"](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Classes) [read page, round 1; fan wiki]
- Detroit: Become Human: choices "can be viewed in a flowchart during and immediately after a given chapter." GameSpot's Peter Brown found the flowchart "ultimately detrimental" to immersion and wished it could be switched off; VideoGamer's Colm Ahern called it one of the game's "few redeeming qualities". — [Wikipedia, "Detroit: Become Human"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit:_Become_Human) [read page, first 100,000 characters]
- Dragon Quest III remakes: the questions route the player to a short playable scenario and behaviour there sets the personality (round 1). — [RPG Site, "How Personalities Work"](https://rpgsite.net/guide/16534-dragon-quest-iii-hd-2d-remake-how-personalities-work) [read page, round 1]

### Inferences
- A per-mission "what you chose" recap is supported by Detroit, but the mixed reviews suggest showing it after the mission, never during, and keeping it dismissible.
- Oblivion and Dragon Quest III are the two precedents for JobQuest's order of events (first mission before Get to know me): the game watches a short stretch of play, then offers a reading the player can refuse.

### Gaps
- Detroit's worldwide percentages and whether locked branches are shown: not in the portion of the article read.
- Telltale's end-of-episode statistics and Fable's alignment: no page opened this session.
- No game with a profile or "identity" page that changes with play was verified beyond Persona 5's rank words.

**Về hình đa giác:** vòng này vẫn không mở được nguồn nào tả màn hình chỉ số của Persona 5, nên câu "Persona 5 vẽ năm chỉ số thành ngũ giác" tiếp tục là chưa kiểm chứng và không nên đưa vào báo cáo trước khi một thành viên chụp màn hình xác nhận. Điều đã kiểm được là Persona 5 truyền đạt chỉ số bằng **năm bậc có tên và giấu điểm thô**, tức thông tin người chơi đọc được nằm ở chữ chứ không ở hình. Bằng chứng này không ủng hộ việc dùng đa giác làm biểu đồ chính; nó hợp với phương án hiện tại là thanh ngang kèm chữ, còn lục giác chỉ làm huy hiệu.
