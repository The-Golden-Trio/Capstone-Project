About the job
Who We Are

Employment Hero is on a mission to make employment easier and more valuable for everyone. Our Employment Operating System brings hiring, HR, payroll and benefits into an all-in-one solution.

Since our inception in 2014, we've scaled to a $2 billion valuation and gained a presence in 6 countries globally - Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Malaysia, the UK and Canada. We now service over 300,000 businesses and more than 2 million employees.

As a Frontend Engineer at Employment Hero, you will

Build and scale product experiences used by thousands of businesses globally
Take ownership of frontend delivery in a fast-moving, high-performance environment
Work in an AI-first engineering culture, leverage AI and agentic ways of working to move faster and solve problems more effectively
Contribute to product and technical direction within a collaborative, remote-first team
Continuously raise the bar on how we build, from code quality to delivery speed and product impact


Who You Are

To thrive at Employment Hero, you'll need to embody The EH Way, operating with focus, agility, and an obsession with impact. For this role, you'll also bring

5+ years in Frontend Engineering with ownership of production systems
Strong in JavaScript/TypeScript, React, and modern build tools
Experience with CI/CD and web performance optimization
Solid understanding of testing, engineering fundamentals, and Agile
Strong English communication for global collaboration
AI-first and agentic mindset, leveraging AI to improve delivery
High ownership, fast execution, adaptable in high-change environments
Open to expanding beyond frontend towards Fullstack
Proactive, able to challenge and contribute in a high-performance team
