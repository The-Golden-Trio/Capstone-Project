# Health, fatigue and stress systems in real games, and how they avoid punishing players

> Researched 3 Nov 2026 for `research/reports/Kinh tế và sức khoẻ JobQuest.md`.
> Fandom, stardewvalleywiki.com and strategywiki blocked fetching; claims from them come from search-result snippets and are marked **(snippet)**. Most sources are fan wikis, guides or forum posts, not developer documents. The best-sourced items are the Persona 3 Reload and Hades rationales.

## Summary

Fatigue and stress systems stay tolerable when four rules hold: the player chooses the risk; the cost is visible before the choice; recovery is cheap and always available; the penalty is a slowdown or lost opportunity, not a loss of score or progress. Strongest single piece of evidence: Atlus removed Persona 3's Fatigue/Sick system in Persona 3 Reload because it clashed with the calendar and hurt enjoyment, and sickness struck at random ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/persona-3-reload-will-remove-fatigue-system/)).

## 1. Persona 3 / 4 / 5

- **P3 (original, FES, Portable): Tired and Sick.** Tired lowers attack and defence; caused by long Tartarus runs, many battles, Full Moon Operations, several nights without sleep. Sick sharply cuts accuracy, evasion and recovery; from entering Tartarus while Tired or from a sick party member. [samurai-gamers](https://samurai-gamers.com/persona-3-reload/p3re-status-conditions-guide/)
- Sick stops the protagonist studying at night. [megatenwiki](https://megamitensei.fandom.com/wiki/Status_Changes_in_Persona_3) (snippet)
- Cures were cheap and varied: nap in class (lose that period's Academics), sleep early, the bathroom, the Tsukaretoreru item, or 3,000 yen at Be Blue V. Tired or Sick also gave bonus Courage at the school clinic, so a debuff became an opportunity. [samurai-gamers](https://samurai-gamers.com/persona-3-reload/p3re-status-conditions-guide/)
- **Removed in Reload**: it clashed with the calendar, random sickness frustrated players who had planned. [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/persona-3-reload-will-remove-fatigue-system/). One guide says conditions still exist; Siliconera treated as stronger.
- P4/P5: no P3-style fatigue found; not verified, do not cite.
- P5 social stat sources: Leblanc curry +1 Guts each time; bathhouse gives Charm, with bonus days; Sunday juice randomly raises one stat. [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/13328-persona-5-royal-stats-guide-increase-knowledge-guts-proficiency-kindness-and-charm-fast)

## 2. The Sims 4

- **Moodlets change skill gain.** Tense slows skill building; Focused speeds Logic, Handiness, Video Gaming, Programming and improves Astronaut and Tech career performance; Energized speeds Fitness; Very Uncomfortable blocks painting, music, writing. [Snooty Sims](https://snootysims.com/wiki/sims-4/the-sims-4-emotions/)
- **Burnout** (Growing Together pack): "Dazed" is the warning sign after long mental or creative focus without breaks; burnout hurts career performance, promotions, skill gain, Fun and Energy gain; any rest helps, the opposite kind of activity helps more. [Sims Community](https://simscommunity.info/2023/05/10/sims-4-burnouts/)
- Burnout cannot be switched off; players asked EA to make it optional. [EA forum](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-4-feedback-en/please-make-burnout-optional/11218467) (player feedback)
- EA moved the Mental Fog trigger from 1–2 days to 3–4 days of the "Work Hard" career tone. [EA forum](https://forums.ea.com/idea/the-sims-4-bug-reports-en/gro-mental-fog-after-one-day-of-hard-work/11880909)
- A "Burnt Out" moodlet from Lifestyles or Dream Home: not found.

## 3. Stardew Valley

- Passing out at 2am or exhausted restores only about half your energy. [wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Passing_out) (snippet)
- Gold loss when collapsing away from home: 10% of money, capped; the cap differs across sources (1,000g vs higher in 1.6). Do not quote a number without checking. [Health](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Health)
- Official-forum players propose gradual exhaustion penalties instead of a hard 2am cutoff, which shows the cutoff feels abrupt. [forum](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/alternative-to-2-00-am-collapse-%E2%80%94-sleep-deprivation-penalties-instead-of-a-hard-cutoff.44773/latest)

## 4. Stress and strain meters

- **Darkest Dungeon.** Stress 0–200; at 100 Afflicted (small chance Virtuous); at 200 heart attack. 5 stress recovered per idle week. Tavern and Abbey remove large amounts, cost gold and keep the hero out for a week; stress capped at 100 on return to town. [Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress_(Darkest_Dungeon)). Abbey costs 1,000–2,700 gold; 37.5% chance of a side effect per visit. [Abbey](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Abbey)
- **Princess Maker 2.** Work and lessons raise stress; too much makes her ill for a month; vacations cost money; illness blocks activity and drops stats. [summary](https://www.arrpeegeez.com/2023/12/princess-maker-2-walkthrough-beginners.html); threshold unclear ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/583040/discussions/0/133257636765618339)).
- **Tokimeki Memorial Girl's Side:** stress ≥ 100 → sick 2 weeks. [fan wiki](https://tokimemogirlsside.fandom.com/wiki/General_Game_Guide_%26_Tips). Original game not verified.
- **Frostpunk.** Extended Shift: +2.5% Discontent per extended workplace, workers skip meals so health worsens. Emergency Shift: +17.5% Discontent for 72 hours and a chance of fatal accidents. [Extended](https://frostpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Extended_Shift_(Adaptation_Law)) · [Emergency](https://frostpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Shift_(Adaptation_Law)) (snippet)
- **Papers, Please.** Mandatory rent; choose between heat, food and medicine; neglect makes the family decline and die; fines out of pay. [wiki](https://papersplease.fandom.com/wiki/Family). The punishing end, useful as contrast.
- **Spent.** $1,000 monthly budget, 30 days, health decisions central; designed to feel hard to win, to build empathy. [Next Avenue](https://www.nextavenue.org/what-online-poverty-game-can-teach-you/) · [PMC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5116791)
- **This War of Mine.** Hungry, tired, sad, sick states feed each other; medicine scarce. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/this-war-of-mine-the-kotaku-review-1660267338)
- **Two Point Hospital.** Staff stamina restored by breaks, staff room, coffee machines; training slows the drain. [Ludo guide](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/two-point-hospital/staff-happiness-staff-threats)
- Cart Life: nothing found.

## 5. Anti-punishment patterns

- **Celeste** Assist Mode was renamed from "Cheat Mode" because the label felt judgmental; shown with a reassuring message. [New Normative](https://newnormative.com/2018/01/25/celestes-assist-mode-brings-welcome-accessibility-options) (secondary quote)
- **Hades God Mode:** 20% damage reduction +2% per death, cap 80%; toggle any time; Kasavin: "it inherently feels bad to die in a game". [Inverse](https://inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview)
- **Soft fail:** a consequence that redirects without ending progress; consistent; not only resource loss. [Wayline](https://www.wayline.io/blog/soft-fails-game-design)
- **Energy systems** without real choices feel arbitrary. [Ed Biden](https://mobilefreetoplay.com/4-steps-to-eliminating-energy/)
- **Disco Elysium** is not a soft-fail model: zero Health or Morale ends the game. [wiki.gg](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Health)

## Takeaways for JobQuest

1. Overtime is an explicit, labelled choice, never automatic or random (Frostpunk laws; P3 Reload removal).
2. Show the cost before the choice as a forecast.
3. Health never touches the score ceiling; it changes turn budget, options, tone, narration.
4. Debuffs change the story or options, not the result.
5. Give every debuff an upside or a narrative hook (P3 clinic Courage).
6. Soft floor: health at zero means an enforced soft rest, not failure.
7. Cap the downside between missions (Darkest Dungeon clamps stress on return).
8. Money recovery is cheap and always available, never a gate.
9. A free fallback always exists (rest, sleep early); money buys speed, not access.
10. No uninvited penalties (Sims burnout complaints).
11. Pair any money cost with narrative change, and keep it recoverable.
12. Escalating visible tiers with warnings (Sims "Dazed").
13. Few, consistent buffs and debuffs.
14. Make the trade genuine: OT sometimes worth taking, sometimes worth refusing.
15. A free reset between missions, framed in the story (a night's sleep).
