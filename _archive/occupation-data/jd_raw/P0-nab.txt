Product Owner - Workplace
About the job
ABOUT THE JOBThis is an opportunity will own and manage the NAB Plus Platform roadmap, backlog and delivery outcomes. The role bridges product strategy, customer experience and technical execution, ensuring the delivery of high-quality digital solutions for NAB colleagues. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to deliver value at speed while maintaining strong alignment with business objectives, platform standards and user needs.
MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES
Define, shape and prioritise the product roadmap, backlog and digital experiences aligned with business objectives
Manage backlog strategy by balancing business priorities, customer needs, platform enhancements and technical improvements
Translate business requirements into epics, features and user stories with clear acceptance criteria and definition of done
Own the end-to-end product lifecycle, ensuring delivery meets quality, security, performance and integration standards
Collaborate with cross-functional squads (engineers, BAs, architects, designers) to deliver roadmap initiatives
Act as the voice of customer and colleague, ensuring solutions are user-centric and value-driven
Facilitate delivery by resolving blockers, managing dependencies and making trade-off decisions
Build and maintain strong stakeholder relationships through clear communication, alignment and expectation management
Provide leadership within the squad to drive outcomes, improve quality and foster continuous improvement
Enable delivery through backlog refinement, sprint planning, decision-making, progress tracking and outcome measurement

YOUR SKILLS & EXPERIENCEMust-haves:Experience
6+ years of experience in digital product environments, experience as a Product Owner or relevant positions.
Strong understanding of Agile methodologies and ways of working (Scrum/Kanban)
Proven experience managing product backlogs and delivering outcomes in cross-functional teams
Ability to translate business needs into clear, actionable user stories
Solid understanding of digital platforms, system integration and product lifecycle
Experience working in complex environments, preferably within financial services or large enterprises
Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to simplify complex challenges
Proven stakeholder management and influencing skills across multiple levels
Strong communication skills in English, both verbal and written

CORE COMPETENCIES:
Customer Impact: Considers customer needs and actions, adds value for the customer.
Business Acumen: Understands business drivers, has financial and commercial orientation.
Relationship Building & Influence: Builds strong relationships and uses influence effectively
