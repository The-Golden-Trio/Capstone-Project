## 1. Question-based character creation in games

### Takeaway
Across thirty years the same four design moves recur: (a) ask dilemmas or oblique questions instead of "what are you like"; (b) keep it to roughly 7–10 questions; (c) present the result as a *suggestion* the player can overrule; (d) let later behaviour, not the quiz, carry the lasting consequences. Games that locked a quiz result in (early Pokémon Mystery Dungeon, Oblivion birthsigns) later added an override or removed the up-front choice; games that promised a background would matter and then barely used it (Cyberpunk 2077 lifepaths) were criticised for exactly that.

**Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar**
- A gypsy fortune-teller using "remotely tarot-like cards of the eight virtues" poses ethical dilemmas; "These situations do not have one correct resolution"; the player in effect ranks the eight virtues and "whichever stands as their highest priority determines the type of character they will play" (e.g. Compassion → Bard, Honor → Paladin, Sacrifice → Tinker). — [Wikipedia, "Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_IV:_Quest_of_the_Avatar) [F, encyclopedia]
- "the gypsy asks seven questions in total"; "The first four have all eight of the Virtues in them, pitting them against each other in pairs"; survivors are paired again until one virtue remains. That virtue sets class, starting location, starting equipment, which companions cannot join, magic ability and allowed gear. — [Ultima Codex, "Character creation"](https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Character_creation) [F] (fan wiki)
- Design intent: players "were intended to feel they were playing themselves transported into a fantasy realm, not a separate person"; Garriott: "Ultima IV was the first one that had ethical overtones in it." Reviewer Scorpia (1987): "for the first time, we have a CRPG whose focus IS character development." — [Wikipedia, same article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_IV:_Quest_of_the_Avatar) [F, encyclopedia]

**Dragon Quest III (personality system, remakes only)**
- "A personality system was added to the remakes"; "A pre-game sequence in which the player answers moral dilemmas similar to that in *Ultima IV* determines the Hero's personality"; "Personalities determine which stats increase when a character levels up"; "The personalities may be changed by use of special items and books." Other party members' personalities come from stat seeds given at creation. — [Wikipedia, "Dragon Quest III"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Quest_III) [F, encyclopedia]
- The quiz is a series of questions followed by a short scenario; the response *in the scenario* sets the final personality. Stats grow "faster or slower depending on that character's 'Personality'" in tiers from "Extremely Quickly" to "Slowly". Accessories change personality only while equipped; books "are single-use items that permanently change a character's Personality". The guide's list contains 46 personalities (count made by the fetch tool from the page's list, not a figure stated by the article). — [RPG Site, "Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake: How Personalities Work"](https://rpgsite.net/guide/16534-dragon-quest-iii-hd-2d-remake-how-personalities-work) [F]
- The Hero's personality is "first determined by how they respond to a Dream Scenario". — [Dragon Quest Wiki, "Personality"](https://dragon-quest.org/wiki/Personality) [F] (fan wiki)
- Question count is disputed between guides: one says 5 random questions lead to one of 8 scenarios and that only the scenario decides; another says the first question is randomised and yes/no answers route to one of eight scenarios. — [Raider King guide](https://raiderking.com/dragon-quest-3-hd-2d-remake-personality-guide-quiz-answers-best-personality-for-hero/); [Dot Esports guide](https://dotesports.com/dragon-quest/news/dragon-quest-3-hd-2d-remake-personality-quiz-answers) [both S-snippet]

**Ogre Battle / Tactics Ogre**

**The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind / IV: Oblivion (class)**

**Until Dawn**

**Cyberpunk 2077 and Mass Effect (backgrounds)**
- Cyberpunk lifepaths "establish V's background and determine their starting location, prologue mission, and initial clothing"; "they unlock unique dialogue options based on V's history" but "do not grant advantages in perks, attributes, or levelling". — [Wikipedia, "Cyberpunk 2077"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberpunk_2077) [F, encyclopedia]
- Critics argued the heavily promoted choice stops mattering after the first mission: occasional background dialogue lines and a single follow-up mission per lifepath. — [TheGamer, "Cyberpunk 2077 life paths pointless"](https://www.thegamer.com/cyberpunk-2077-life-paths-pointless/) [S-snippet; fetch failed]
- Mass Effect: Pre-Service History gives one unique quest each and is "referred to in minor ways throughout all three games"; Psychological Profile sets starting Paragon / Renegade points and some dialogue. The guide calls the options "broadly equal" and says "there is no right or wrong choice here." — [RPG Site, "Mass Effect Service History & Psych Profile"](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/11115-mass-effect-service-history-psych-profile-how-to-choose-the-best-background) [F]

### Inferences
- **Indirection is the norm.** None of the quiz games asks "are you patient?". They ask what you would *do* (Ultima IV, DQ3's scenario, Morrowind) or something whose scoring cannot be guessed (Pottermore's "Dawn or Dusk?", Fallout's Rorschach). DQ3 goes furthest: the questions are routing, and the *behaviour in a playable scenario* is the measurement. This is the closest precedent for JobQuest's stance that play, not self-report, is the core (D-03).
- **Suggestion plus override became the standard.** Morrowind (2002), Fallout 3 (2008) and New Vegas (2010) all let the player overrule the quiz; Pokémon Mystery Dungeon added an override in later entries. A profile that says "this is our reading, edit it" has long precedent and matches D-02 (never "you fit job X").
- **The result must visibly come back.** Until Dawn and Shattered Memories echo the player's own answers back later (a prop, a changed character); Cyberpunk was criticised when the choice faded. For JobQuest: something the player wrote in Get to know me should reappear in a later scenario or in the profile summary, quoted or clearly recognisable.
- **"Earned" stats read as more legitimate than "declared" stats.** Persona 5's five stats and Oblivion's behaviour-based class proposal both derive from actions. A self-declared radar chart should start faint or provisional and be overwritten by play signals.
- **Inaccurate instant feedback hurts trust** (PopMatters on Shattered Memories' "quickie profiles"): a short AI summary after five minutes of answers risks the same reaction, so it should be hedged and editable.

### Gaps
- Whether Ultima IV's question text names the two virtues being weighed: the Codex page did not say, and I did not open a transcript of the questions.
- Fandom wikis (Nukapedia, Megami Tensei Wiki, Final Fantasy Wiki), Bulbapedia, fallout.wiki and megatenwiki.com all refused automated access (402/403). The G.O.A.T. question count, the New Vegas test sequence, the PMD question counts and Avellone's statements are therefore snippet-level only.
- No source read states that Persona 5 draws the five stats as a pentagon; treat the "ngũ giác" detail in the plan as unverified.
- Whether J.K. Rowling personally wrote the Sorting questions: no primary source opened.
- No developer post-mortem or GDC talk specifically on quiz-based character creation was found; player reception is from reviews, not survey data.
- The Sims 4: whether traits can be changed later was not covered by the source read.

## 2. Research on character creation, identification, onboarding length and player motivation

### Takeaway
The best-supported account of why profile building can feel like play is self-determination theory: autonomy, competence and relatedness independently predict enjoyment and future play, and customising an avatar raises identification with it. Player "types" are weak science by their own author's admission; factor-analytic motivation models replaced them. The one hard onboarding number found is Duolingo's: moving sign-up after a first lesson raised daily active users by about 20%.

### Cited Findings
- **Bartle (1996)** proposes achievers, explorers, socialisers and killers on two axes, "action versus interaction, and world-oriented versus player-oriented". The source was a bulletin-board debate "from November 1989 to May 1990" in which "Some 15 individuals took a major part, with perhaps another 15 adding their comments from time to time". Bartle warns he is "not a trained psychologist, do not expect a conventionally rigorous approach", and that the types describe "*stereotypical* players". — [Bartle, "Hearts, Clubs, Diamonds, Spades: Players Who Suit MUDs"](https://mud.co.uk/richard/hcds.htm) [F]
- **Yee (2006)**: "a factor analytic approach was used to create an empirical model of player motivations. The analysis revealed 10 motivation subcomponents that grouped into 3 overarching components (Achievement, Social, and Immersion)." The introduction notes that the assumptions of Bartle's model "have never been empirically tested". — [Yee, "Motivations of Play in Online Games", CyberPsychology & Behavior 9, 772–775 (author's PDF)](http://www.nickyee.com/pubs/Yee%20-%20Motivations%20(2007).pdf) [A + introduction read]
- **Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski (2006)**: "Four studies apply self-determination theory"; in Studies 1–3 "perceived in-game autonomy and competence are associated with game enjoyment, preferences, and changes in well-being pre- to post-play"; "Competence and autonomy perceptions are also related to the intuitive nature of game controls"; in Study 4 "SDT's theorized needs for autonomy, competence, and relatedness independently predict enjoyment and future game play." They cite Rigby's view that typologies "largely reflect the structure and content of current games, rather than the fundamental or underlying motives". — [Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski, "The Motivational Pull of Video Games", Motivation and Emotion (PDF)](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf) [A + first two pages read]
- **Quantic Foundry**: "proposed a model comprising 12 distinct motives belonging to six higher-order motivation clusters", "tested on more than 500,000 gamers worldwide"; criticised because "it is not openly accessible to the scientific community due to copyright protection" and because it dropped escapism. Yee's 2006 model is criticised as "developed primarily for MMORPG players". — [Király et al. (2022), "A comprehensive model to understand and assess the motivational background of video game use: The Gaming Motivation Inventory", Journal of Behavioral Addictions 11(3)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9872527) [F, first part of article]
- **Avatar customisation and identification**: in a lab study with Lord of the Rings Online (N = 66, about ten hours over two weeks, customisation vs no-customisation groups), both time and avatar-based customisation positively affected players' identification with their avatars. — [Turkay & Kinzer, "The Effects of Avatar-Based Customization on Player Identification" (chapter abstract, IRMA)](https://www.irma-international.org/chapter/the-effects-of-avatar-based-customization-on-player-identification/126061) [S-snippet of abstract]
- Later CHI work summarises Birk, Atkins, Bowey & Mandryk (CHI 2016, "Fostering intrinsic motivation through avatar identification in digital games", DOI 10.1145/2858036.2858062) as showing that higher avatar identification raises motivation. — [Kao et al., CHI 2022 (PDF)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/dkao/pdf/kao2022chi.pdf) [S-snippet; original paper not opened]
- **Onboarding order (Duolingo)**: letting people do a lesson before asking them to register produced "about a 20% increase in DAUs"; later tuning of three skippable "soft walls" plus one hard wall added a further 8.2%; without the soft walls the hard wall "perform[s] significantly worse". Source: Gina Gotthilf, VP of Growth. — [First Round Review, "The Tenets of A/B Testing from Duolingo's Master Growth Hacker"](https://review.firstround.com/the-tenets-of-a-b-testing-from-duolingos-master-growth-hacker) [F]
- **Typing on phones**: survey-methods studies report shorter open-ended answers on smartphones and higher break-off (one paradata study: smartphone respondents 2.6 times as likely to break off as tablet / PC respondents; a German experiment found the content of smartphone answers still meaningful, only shorter). — [Survey Practice paper (PDF)](https://www.surveypractice.org/article/84347.pdf); [SSOAR record of the German salary-site experiment](https://www.ssoar.info/ssoar/handle/document/71616) [both S-snippet; survey context, not games]

### Inferences
- **What makes profile building feel like play rather than a form** (synthesis of the above with section 1): the player makes choices that are theirs (autonomy: optional, skippable, editable, overridable result), gets an immediate visible consequence (competence: the profile visibly changes after each answer), and the system "answers back" in the fiction (relatedness: a character reacts, as with Dr. Hill or Warren). A form has none of the three.
- **Do not build the profile on player "types".** Bartle's own caveats, Yee's and Ryan et al.'s criticisms, and the proprietary status of Quantic Foundry all point the same way as the report's criticism of MBTI labels: use continuous dimensions, not types.
- **Sequence matters more than length.** The Duolingo result supports what D-29 already implies: let the user play a first mission before Get to know me, and offer it as a skippable "soft wall" more than once instead of a single gate.
- **Budget typing.** With answers shorter and drop-off higher on phones, a free-text-first flow should cap the number of typed answers (single digits) and accept one or two sentences.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study was found that measures drop-off as a function of *character-creation or onboarding questionnaire length* in games. The Duolingo figure concerns sign-up placement, not questionnaire length, and is a company self-report of DAU, not retention.
- Turkay & Kinzer and Birk et al. were not read in full; effect sizes unknown.
- Quantic Foundry's own pages returned 403; the sample figure is from Király et al., and other publications cite different totals.
- No GDC talk or post-mortem on first-time user experience was opened.

## 3. Consumer products: patterns for eliciting and presenting a self

### Takeaway
The transferable patterns are: one small "story" per screen built from the user's own behaviour (Spotify), prompts that are replaced when they under-perform and coached toward specific detail without rewriting the user's words (Hinge), streak and contribution displays that reward showing up (Duolingo, GitHub), depth that increases in levels (We're Not Really Strangers), and an AI that remembers and asks follow-ups (Rosebud). 16Personalities shows the opposite risk: an attractive type label over a model its own maker says must not be read as destiny.

### Cited Findings
- **Spotify Wrapped, structure**: each statistic is treated as a separate "data story" ("we will refer to each of these separate statistics as a 'data story'"); stories are computed by separate jobs and stored per user, so one story can be changed without redoing the rest. — [Spotify Engineering, "Spotify Unwrapped: How we brought you a decade of data" (2020)](https://engineering.atspotify.com/2020/2/spotify-unwrapped-how-we-brought-you-a-decade-of-data) [F]
- **Spotify Wrapped 2022, a type system built from behaviour**: "we're showcasing 16 different Listening Personality types that Spotify created for 2022 Wrapped"; "It's not only about what you listened to—but what that says about your music taste." Also "Audio Day", "An interactive story that gives you a peek into how your music taste evolved throughout the day", plus share cards. — [Spotify Newsroom, "Everything You Need To Know About 2022 Wrapped", 30 Nov 2022](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2022-11-30/everything-you-need-to-know-about-2022-wrapped/) [F]
- The 16 types come from four two-sided measures of listening behaviour: Familiarity / Exploration, Loyalty / Variety, Timelessness / Newness, Commonality / Uniqueness. — [Spotify Newsroom, tag "Your Listening Personality"](https://newsroom.spotify.com/tag/your-listening-personality) [S-snippet]
- **Hinge prompts**: "likes on prompt responses are 47% more likely to lead to a date than likes on photos"; with psychotherapist Esther Perel, Hinge added "My World" prompts, "a collection of 10 questions designed to help users share more about themselves", "replacing 10 underperforming prompts"; Gen Z is 56% of Hinge's base. — [Fast Company, "Hinge is teaming up with Esther Perel to rethink dating prompts" (read via TheNote repost, June 2025)](https://thenote.app/post/en/hinge-is-teaming-up-with-esther-perel-to-rethink-dating-prompts-cp86pdi3bf) [S: repost of the article, read in full]
- **Hinge Prompt Feedback (AI coaching on free-text answers)**: nearly 40% of Hinge users say responding to prompts is "very difficult"; the feature asks for more vivid, specific detail or a small tonal change, "favoring light, encouraging feedback with no auto-rewrites"; design principles Transparency, Authenticity, Equity; early prototypes over-explained, softened jokes, or pushed "add more" where understatement was intended, and were tuned back. "The feature is there to explore your voice, not replace it." — [Hinge Newsroom, Kwok & Vanderbilt, "How Prompt Feedback Helps You Get Unstuck While Keeping Your Voice", 25 Nov 2025](https://hinge.co/newsroom/about-prompt-feedback) [F]
- **Duolingo streak**: "there are over 6 million people on a streak of 7 days or more"; learners "who reach a streak of just 7 days are 3.6 times more likely to complete their course"; milestone animations raised day-7 retention of new learners by +1.7%; allowing two Streak Freezes raised daily active learners by +0.38% without more days off. — [Duolingo Blog, Osman Mansur, "The habit-building research behind your Duolingo streak", 31 Jan 2022](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/) [F]
- **GitHub profile**: a contribution calendar for the past year, up to six pinned repositories, an optional activity overview, achievements; by default only public activity counts and private activity, if shown, is anonymised. — [GitHub Docs, "Contributions on your profile"](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/concepts/contributions-on-your-profile) [F]
- **Rosebud (AI journal)**: "Rosebud remembers and can help you make sense of it all"; "Receive in-depth reports highlighting your progress every week"; a user review: "The AI version asks me unlimited feedback questions." These are marketing claims and testimonials; the page gives no outcome data. — [Rosebud home page](https://www.rosebud.app/) [F, vendor marketing]
- **We're Not Really Strangers**: three levels, Perception (first impressions and assumptions), Connection (personal sharing), Reflection (looking back on the conversation); play ends with each player writing the other a note. — [Stanford course blog "Mechanics of Magic", "Critical Play: We're Not Really Strangers", 28 Apr 2023](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2023/04/28/critical-play-bluffing-judging-and-getting-vulnerable-were-not-really-strangers/) [S-snippet; official how-to-play page returned 404]
- **16Personalities**: describes its own "NERIS" framework as five independent trait spectrums; the first four give the familiar four-letter code and the fifth, Identity, adds the Assertive / Turbulent suffix (e.g. INTJ-A / INTJ-T); it keeps the Myers-Briggs letter format without Jungian cognitive functions; its guidance says results should not be presented as a diagnosis, a fixed destiny or a deterministic prediction. — [16Personalities, "AI Reference" page](https://www.16personalities.com/for-ai) [S-snippet; site returned 403]

### Inferences
- **One card, one fact, drawn from behaviour** (Spotify) is a direct template for the profile's "few stats": each stat is an independent, replaceable story ("Bạn đã thử 4 nghề trong 2 tuần"), which also fits the existing event-sourced `EventAnswer` data.
- **Spotify's Listening Personality is a cautionary twin of MBTI**: four binary axes → 16 labels, but computed from a year of behaviour rather than self-report. If JobQuest ever shows a label, the defensible version is behaviour-derived, presented as a description of *how you played so far*, never as a type you are.
- **Hinge's coaching rules map onto the AI follow-up question**: ask for one concrete detail, never rewrite, never push "add more" by default, test across writing styles. Hinge's own 40% "very difficult" figure is a warning that open prompts are hard for many users even when motivated.
- **Curate prompts by performance** (Hinge replaced its ten weakest): log skip rate and answer length per Get to know me prompt and retire the weak ones.
- **Depth in levels** (WNRS): start with low-stakes perception questions, move to personal ones, end with a reflection that reuses what was said earlier.
- **Privacy default** (GitHub): private activity hidden unless the user opts in, consistent with BR-06.

### Gaps
- No published Hinge data on *which* prompts lead to dates was found; third-party blogs quote figures without a traceable Hinge source, so I left them out.

## 4. Astrology: evidence, belief, and why people enjoy it

### Takeaway
Controlled tests are consistently negative: in Carlson's double-blind test astrologers matched charts to personality profiles at 0.34 against a chance rate of 0.33 and their own predicted 0.5, and a 173,709-person dataset found no link between sign and personality. Belief is nonetheless common among the young (37% of US adults under 30; in a 2017 YouGov poll Vietnam had the highest share in Asia-Pacific taking horoscopes seriously, 75%), and most US users say they do it "just for fun". The appeal is psychological (self-verification, coping, flattering descriptions), and it is not harmless: positive or negative horoscopes measurably shift performance, and sign stereotypes produce discrimination.

### Cited Findings

**Controlled tests**
- Carlson (1985), Part 1: if selection were random, subjects would pick their own natal-chart interpretation "one third of the time"; the astrologers predicted "at least half". Part 2: 116 envelopes returned; astrologers "selected the correct natal chart as their first place choice at the 0.34 ± 0.044 rate, in agreement with the scientific hypothesis of 0.33 and in disagreement with the astrological hypothesis of 0.5 by 3.3 standard deviations". Conclusion: "The experiment clearly refutes the astrological hypothesis." — [Carlson, "A double-blind test of astrology", Nature 318, 419–425 (5 Dec 1985), PDF](https://muller.lbl.gov/papers/Astrology-Carlson.pdf) [F: pages 419, 424 and 425 read]
- 28 astrologers took part, 26 of them nominated by the National Council for Geocosmic Research. A meta-analysis pooled "40 studies consisting of 700 astrologers and over 1,000 birth charts"; a study of "2011 sets of people born within 5 minutes of each other" found no effect; agreement between astrologers across 25 studies was about 0.1. — [Wikipedia, "Astrology and science"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrology_and_science) [S, encyclopedia read in full]
- Wyman & Vyse (2008): 52 students could identify their real NEO-FFI profile above chance but not their real astrological summary; a Barnum effect appeared in accuracy ratings of both. — [Discover Magazine, "Are personality tests any more accurate than astrology?"](https://discovermagazine.com/mind/psychology-vs-astrology) [S-snippet; Journal of General Psychology 135(3), DOI 10.3200/GENP.135.3.287-300 not opened]
- Lu, Liu, Liao & Wang (2020): "Astrological sign predicted neither personality (N = 173,709) nor job performance (N = 32,878)." — [Lu et al., "Disentangling Stereotypes From Social Reality: Astrological Stereotypes and Discrimination in China", JPSP 119(6) (PDF)](https://gosling.psy.utexas.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Lu-et-al.-2020-Disentangling-stereotypes-from-social-reality-Astrological-stereotypes-and-discrimination-in-China-JPSP.pdf) [A: abstract read on the paper's first page]

**Barnum effect and self-attribution**
- Forer (1949) gave every student the same sketch, taken from an astrology book, as if individual; the average accuracy rating was 4.2 out of 5. Believers rate generic profiles as more accurate than sceptics do. People who know their sign describe themselves in line with it, more so when told the study is about astrology; people unfamiliar with sun-sign lore do not. — [Wikipedia, "Astrology and science"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrology_and_science) [S, encyclopedia read in full]

**Belief and use**
- United States, Pew (fielded 21–27 Oct 2024, n = 9,593): 30% consult astrology, tarot or a fortune teller at least yearly (28% astrology / horoscopes); 20% do so mostly "just for fun" against 10% for "helpful insights"; 27% believe in astrology (29% in 2017); 43% of women aged 18–49 believe; 1% rely on these practices "a lot" for major life decisions. — [Pew Research Center, "3 in 10 Americans consult astrology, tarot cards or fortune tellers", 21 May 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/21/3-in-10-americans-consult-astrology-tarot-cards-or-fortune-tellers/) [F]
- United States, YouGov (21–22 Apr 2022, n = 3,472): 27% believe in astrology, 37% of under-30s, 16% of those 65 and over; 82% of under-30s know their sign. — [YouGov, "One in four Americans say they believe in astrology", 26 Apr 2022](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/42292-one-four-americans-say-they-believe-astrology) [F]
- **Asia-Pacific including Vietnam**, YouGov (online, 14–29 Aug 2017, n = 9,645 across nine markets; Vietnam n = 1,003): 43% take horoscopes very or somewhat seriously, 48% of 16–29s and 31% of over-45s; Vietnam highest at 75%; 33% had changed their behaviour because of a horoscope. — [YouGov, "New YouGov research reveals nearly half of young people take star signs seriously", 18 Oct 2017](https://yougov.com/articles/19426-horoscope-and-behaviour-change) [F]
- Pew's 35-country report: in most countries "fewer than a quarter of adults" consult fortune tellers or horoscopes (India 45%); the page read does not mention Vietnam. — [Pew Research Center, "Spells, curses and ways to see the future", 6 May 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/06/spells-curses-and-ways-to-see-the-future/) [F, overview page]
- Vietnam, religious context (face-to-face, Jun–Sep 2023): 48% identify with no religion, yet 86% made offerings to ancestors in the past year and 79% say an ancestor's spirit has come to their aid. — [Pew Research Center, "Religion and Spirituality in East Asian Societies", 17 Jun 2024](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2024/06/17/religion-and-spirituality-in-east-asian-societies/) [F]

**Why people enjoy or use it**
- Students taking an astrology course (unlike those in psychology or German) became more certain of their self-descriptions; interest in astrology tracked the number of personal crises reported. — [Lillqvist & Lindeman (1998), "Belief in Astrology as a Strategy For Self-Verification and Coping With Negative Life-Events", European Psychologist 3(3)](https://econtent.hogrefe.com/doi/10.1027/1016-9040.3.3.202) [S-snippet of abstract; publisher page returned 403]
- In an online sample (N = 264), "Narcissism was surprisingly the strongest predictor" of belief in astrology; intelligence related negatively. — [Andersson, Persson & Kajonius (2022), "Even the stars think that I am superior", Personality and Individual Differences 187 (Lund University portal)](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/publications/even-the-stars-think-that-i-am-superior-personality-intelligence-) [A]
- Reading a positive rather than negative horoscope increased positive interpretation of ambiguous events (N = 195), cognitive performance (N = 189) and creativity (N = 193); effects on performance and creativity were stronger for people with low internal locus of control. — [Clobert, Van Cappellen, Bourdon & Cohen (2016), "Good day for Leos", Personality and Individual Differences 101, 348–355 (ASU portal)](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/good-day-for-leos-horoscopes-influence-on-perception-cognitive-pe) [A]

### Inferences
- The evidence the advisor will expect is short and strong: Carlson (1985) for the direct test, Lu et al. (2020) for scale, Forer for why descriptions still feel accurate. These three also back the report's existing argument against type labels, so a zodiac feature can be framed as an *application* of that argument rather than a contradiction of it, provided it makes no trait claim.
- "For fun" is the majority mode of use in the US (20% vs 10%), but the YouGov Vietnam figure (75% "seriously") suggests a Vietnamese audience may take sign content more literally than the team assumes. A "chỉ để vui" label is necessary but probably not sufficient; content must be harmless even when believed.
- Clobert et al. means a horoscope-style "buff" text is not inert: telling a player their sign is "weak at detail" could depress performance in a scored scenario. Any text must be positive or neutral and identical in valence across signs.
- The self-attribution effect means sign descriptions could contaminate the free-text self-description the profile is trying to collect (players describing themselves as their sign "should" be). Ask Get to know me questions *before* showing any sign content.

### Gaps
- No recent, methodologically documented survey of astrology belief among Vietnamese youth was found. The YouGov figure is from 2017, an online panel, and "horoscope" may have been understood to include Eastern tử vi.
- Hamilton (2001) on favourable signs and belief, Hartmann et al. (2006), Dean & Kelly (2003) and Wyman & Vyse (2008) were not opened in the original.
- US National Science Board data on how many young adults regard astrology as "scientific" was located only through a press snippet and is omitted.
- The 2024 Clearer Thinking astrologer test: the page returned 404, so it is not cited.

## 5. Birth-sign and zodiac mechanics in games

### Takeaway
Games have used a birth sign in three ways: as a hidden combat modifier tied to the player's real birthday (Final Fantasy Tactics), as a permanent stat choice (Morrowind, Oblivion) that Skyrim turned into a perk the player can switch at any time, and as pure flavour (Animal Crossing). The design history runs toward choice and reversibility: a permanent advantage fixed at the start produced balance complaints and restarts.

### Cited Findings
- **Final Fantasy Tactics**: "Every character in Ivalice has a Zodiac sign based on their birth month"; the protagonist's follows the birthday entered at the start of a new game. "A positive affinity means abilities inflict more damage on one another, heal for a greater amount, and with better accuracy"; "Negative affinity has the opposite effect." The chart has Positive, Negative and Best columns, and "Best" does not apply to creatures. — [RPG Site, "Final Fantasy Tactics – The Ivalice Chronicles Zodiac Compatibility Guide"](https://www.rpgsite.net/guide/18604-final-fantasy-tactics-the-ivalice-chronicles-zodiac-compatibility-guide-chart-signs-list) [F]
- **Morrowind**: 13 birthsigns (3 Guardians, the Serpent, 9 Charges); several carry trade-offs (Atronach: large magicka pool but "they cannot generate magicka of their own"; Apprentice: "more vulnerable to magick as well"). Birthsign is the last choice in character generation, before a final confirmation screen. — [UESP, "Morrowind:Birthsigns"](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Birthsigns); [UESP, "Morrowind:Character Creation"](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Character_Creation) [both F] (fan wiki)
- **Oblivion**: 13 birthsigns, picked mid-tutorial with one last chance before leaving the sewers; afterwards the sign "cannot be changed in-game (without the use of the console)". Balance is uneven: the Thief gives 30 attribute points against 20 for other attribute signs; the Shadow's daily invisibility is weaker than a power available elsewhere, "making this birthsign of limited value". — [UESP, "Oblivion:Birthsigns"](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Birthsigns) [F] (fan wiki)
- **Skyrim**: "There are thirteen Standing Stones in total, each associated with one of Tamriel's thirteen constellations"; "Usually, only one stone's effects can be active at a time"; "you can change which stone is active at any time by activating a different Standing Stone." — [UESP, "Skyrim:Standing Stones"](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Standing_Stones) [F] (fan wiki)
- Why Skyrim dropped up-front commitments (stated about classes): Todd Howard said Oblivion players chose after about half an hour, then after about three hours realised they had picked the wrong skills and wanted to start over, and the team asked whether there was a way to solve that. — [TechRaptor, "Sets of Skills mod gives Skyrim some class" (relaying a 2011 Game Informer podcast)](https://techraptor.net/gaming/features/sets-of-skills-mod-gives-skyrim-some-class) [S-snippet; page returned 403]
- **Final Fantasy XII International Zodiac Job System**: "twelve license boards (instead of the original game's one), each corresponding to a different Zodiac sign and job." The Zodiac Age remaster scores 86/100 on Metacritic (PS4). Here the zodiac is a naming theme for jobs, not a birth mechanic. — [Wikipedia, "Final Fantasy XII"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XII) [F, encyclopedia]
- **Animal Crossing**: players set a birthday (in New Horizons, at character creation); villagers give gifts and a party. "The player's star sign is displayed on their Town Pass Card" (New Leaf) and on the Passport (New Horizons); villagers may comment on it. A "furniture piece corresponding to each sign are distributed during the month for each star sign", in New Horizons "as DIY recipes from Celeste", and "star signs affect the number of star fragments that the player can get by favoring zodiac fragments". No compatibility or advantage mechanic is described. — [Nookipedia, "Birthday"](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Birthday); [Nookipedia, "Star sign"](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Star_sign) [both F] (fan wiki)
- **Genshin Impact**: "constellations" are per-character upgrade tiers unlocked with a Stella Fortuna item that is obtained only by pulling a duplicate of that character from the gacha; they are unrelated to the player's birth date, and are the focus of pay-to-win criticism. — [Fextralife wiki, "Constellations"](https://genshinimpact.wiki.fextralife.com/Constellations) [S-snippet] (fan wiki)

### Inferences
- **Three tiers of risk.** Cosmetic and calendar-based (Animal Crossing) has no fairness problem: everyone gets the same items in the same month and the sign is a line on a passport. Chosen and switchable (Skyrim) has a mild balance problem only. Birth-date-fixed with numeric effect (FFT, Oblivion) creates advantages nobody chose, and this is the one category JobQuest's BR-02 already rules out.
- **Animal Crossing's "season" model is symmetric by construction**: the zodiac drives *what everyone gets this month*, not *what you get because of when you were born*. That is the cleanest way to give a "buff" without birth-date unfairness.
- **Skyrim's fix is the lesson for any zodiac perk**: decouple the perk from birth and let the player change it. The birth date can pre-select a default; it should not lock anything.
- Oblivion's uneven signs show that even "small" bonuses get ranked by players; twelve perks that differ in value will be min-maxed and resented. Either make them identical in value or purely cosmetic.

### Gaps
- **Final Fantasy Tactics numbers are unverified.** The Final Fantasy Wiki (402), StrategyWiki (403), FFHacktics wiki (403) and GameFAQs (connection reset) could not be opened. Snippets give two conflicting ladders (best ×1.5 / good ×1.25 / bad ×0.75 / worst ×0.5 versus a different ordering) and an unconfirmed gender rule. Do not quote multipliers in the report without checking a primary guide. No source on player controversy around the system was opened.
- No direct statement from Bethesda about *birthsigns* specifically was found; the Howard remark concerns classes and is snippet-level.
- FFXII: whether a job choice was permanent and how The Zodiac Age changed that is not in the article read.
- The Sims' zodiac signs (derived from personality points in early games): only low-quality mirror pages surfaced; not cited.
- Whether Animal Crossing's zodiac fragments depend on the current date or on the player's own sign is ambiguous in the sentence quoted; check the page before relying on it.

## 6. Vietnamese cultural fit: con giáp, ngũ hành, cung hoàng đạo

### Takeaway
The Vietnamese twelve-animal cycle differs from the Chinese in two animals (water buffalo for ox, cat for rabbit) and, unlike Western signs among the young, it has measurable real-world weight: families have timed births to auspicious years. No trustworthy Vietnamese survey on youth use of Western zodiac signs exists in what I could find; local press describes a visible Gen Z fortune-telling trend but cites no numbers or recycles a US survey.

### Cited Findings
- The Vietnamese zodiac follows the Chinese one except that "the water buffalo replaces the ox, and the cat replaces the rabbit"; the signs are Tý, Sửu, Dần, Mão, Thìn, Tỵ, Ngọ, Mùi, Thân, Dậu, Tuất, Hợi; years are grouped in 60-year cycles, "Each of these consists of five 12-year animal cycles". The article's explanation for the cat (sound similarity between the rabbit sign's character and "mèo") rests on user-generated Wiktionary content. — [Wikipedia, "Vietnamese zodiac"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_zodiac) [F, encyclopedia; cat explanation weakly sourced]
- Do & Phung use the Vietnamese belief that birth dates shape success, luck, character and compatibility; for 1976–1996 they find birth cohorts in auspicious years are significantly larger, and children born in those years do better in health and education. — [Do & Phung, "Superstition, Family Planning, and Human Development", World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 4001](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9526441d-7896-55cd-ae40-a7ceaa8734a2/content) [S-snippet of abstract; World Bank page returned 403]
- 2012 (dragon year): the Health Ministry reported over 500,000 births in the first five months, up 13.5% on the previous year. — [UCA News, "Auspicious year prompts baby boom"](https://www.ucanews.com/amp/auspicious-year-prompts-baby-boom/56801) [S-snippet]
- 2024 (dragon year) did not repeat this: the fertility rate fell to 1.91 from 1.96. — [Vietcetera, "The Dragon Slumbers: Vietnamese Marriage And Birth Rates Hit Historic Lows", Feb 2025](https://vietcetera.com/en/the-dragon-slumbers-vietnamese-marriage-and-birth-rates-hit-historic-lows) [S-snippet]
- Vietnamese press on Gen Z: the Sài Gòn Giải Phóng piece (15 Mar 2025) describes young people consuming tarot, astrology and card readings through TikTok and Instagram, refers only to "những con số thống kê không chính thức", and quotes unnamed sociologists warning about dependence. It contains no survey figure. — [Sài Gòn Giải Phóng, Trọng Trung, "Bói toán và gen Z"](https://www.sggp.org.vn/boi-toan-va-gen-z-post786053.html) [F]
- The Znews piece on using birth charts to choose jobs (25 Aug 2024) is entirely based on a US survey of 2,000 young Americans by the essay platform EduBirdie (e.g. about 63% say astrology affects their careers); it has no Vietnamese data. — [Znews, "Thế hệ xem bản đồ sao để tìm việc"](https://lifestyle.zingnews.vn/the-he-xem-ban-do-sao-de-tim-viec-post1493879.html) [F]
- Vietnam ranked highest of nine Asia-Pacific markets for taking horoscopes seriously (75%, n = 1,003, online, 2017). — [YouGov, 18 Oct 2017](https://yougov.com/articles/19426-horoscope-and-behaviour-change) [F]

### Inferences
- **Con giáp is birth-*year* based**, so nearly every user in the same school year shares one animal. It cannot differentiate classmates, and it carries family-level beliefs about compatibility and luck (Do & Phung). That makes it both less useful as a personal flavour layer and higher-risk than the Western twelve signs, which are birth-*month* based and circulate among the young mainly as social-media entertainment.
- If a Vietnamese touch is wanted, the safest use of con giáp is calendar flavour (the current lunar year's animal as a seasonal theme for everyone), not a per-user attribute.
- A widely repeated "62% of Gen Z believe their sign reflects their personality" figure in Vietnamese marketing articles has no traceable source and should not be used.

### Gaps
- No source was read on ngũ hành (five elements), tam hợp or tứ hành xung; nothing here should be cited for them.
- No Vietnamese survey organisation's data (e.g. Decision Lab, Q&Me, Viện Nghiên cứu Thanh niên) on zodiac use was found.
- The Do & Phung paper and the dragon-year press figures were not opened in full.

## 7. Risks and ethics of astrology in an educational product, and mitigating framings

### Takeaway
Three risks are evidenced, not hypothetical: credibility (the science is negative and the report already attacks type labels), behavioural influence (horoscope text shifts performance; knowing your sign shapes self-description), and fairness (sign stereotypes produce discrimination, and birth-fixed bonuses are unchosen advantages). The mitigations with precedent are opt-in, calendar-symmetric or self-chosen effects, cosmetic-only rewards, and never attaching trait or suitability claims to a sign.

### Cited Findings
- Sign stereotypes cause real discrimination even though they are false: in China Virgos "are stereotyped as having disagreeable personalities", which "led Chinese individuals to discriminate against Virgos in romantic dating (Study 4) and in simulated job recruitment (Studies 5 and 6)", while sign "predicted neither personality (N = 173,709) nor job performance (N = 32,878)". — [Lu et al. (2020), JPSP (PDF)](https://gosling.psy.utexas.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Lu-et-al.-2020-Disentangling-stereotypes-from-social-reality-Astrological-stereotypes-and-discrimination-in-China-JPSP.pdf) [A]
- Horoscope valence changes cognitive performance and creativity, more strongly for people with low internal locus of control. — [Clobert et al. (2016), PAID 101 (ASU portal)](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/good-day-for-leos-horoscopes-influence-on-perception-cognitive-pe) [A]
- People who know their sign's supposed traits describe themselves accordingly, especially when the astrology context is made salient. — [Wikipedia, "Astrology and science"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrology_and_science) [S, encyclopedia]
- Most US users treat it as entertainment (20% "just for fun" vs 10% "helpful insights"), and only 1% rely on it "a lot" for major decisions. — [Pew Research Center, 21 May 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/21/3-in-10-americans-consult-astrology-tarot-cards-or-fortune-tellers/) [F]
- Precedents for low-risk framing: Animal Crossing shows the sign on a passport and hands out sign-themed items to everyone during each sign's month ([Nookipedia, "Star sign"](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Star_sign) [F] (fan wiki)); Skyrim lets the player switch constellation perk "at any time" ([UESP, "Skyrim:Standing Stones"](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Standing_Stones) [F] (fan wiki)); Cyberpunk lifepaths "do not grant advantages in perks, attributes, or levelling" ([Wikipedia, "Cyberpunk 2077"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberpunk_2077) [F]).
- Even a vendor of a type-label product instructs that results should not be presented as a diagnosis, fixed destiny or deterministic prediction. — [16Personalities, "AI Reference"](https://www.16personalities.com/for-ai) [S-snippet]

### Inferences
- **Credibility**: the feature is defensible to the advisor only if it makes *no* claim that the sign says anything about the person. Constellation mythology and astronomy are facts about the sky; "Bạch Dương thì nóng vội" is a personality claim and belongs with the MBTI labels the report criticises.
- **Fairness**: a perk fixed by date of birth is an advantage assigned by an attribute the user cannot change; it conflicts with BR-02 in spirit even if it avoids competence points. Symmetric-by-calendar or self-chosen-and-switchable perks avoid this.
- **Career guidance context**: because the product is about occupations, any sentence linking a sign to a job or a work trait reproduces, in miniature, the Virgo effect documented by Lu et al. This should be a hard rule for AI-written text: the narrator model must not be given the user's sign as context when writing scenario feedback or the self-summary.
- **Asking "do you believe in astrology?"**: the answer is a belief statement about a minor in many cases; storing it adds personal data with no mechanic that needs it. Asking "Bật lớp chòm sao (cho vui)?" collects a preference instead of a belief and achieves the same opt-out.
- **Order of exposure**: show nothing sign-related until after Get to know me, to keep self-descriptions uncontaminated.

### Gaps
- No published guidance or case study was found on astrology features in *educational* software specifically; the argument above is assembled from adjacent evidence.
- No evidence was found on whether an explicit "for fun" label changes how users interpret sign content.
- Legal classification of belief data or birth-date-derived attributes under Vietnamese personal-data law was not researched here (plan item 07-E covers it).

## Đề xuất cho JobQuest

### A. Mẫu nên mượn cho Get to know me

1. **Hỏi bằng tình huống phải đánh đổi, không hỏi thẳng "bạn là người thế nào"** (Ultima IV: 7 câu, mỗi câu hai đức tính đối nhau; Dragon Quest III: câu hỏi chỉ dẫn đường, thứ được đọc là cách người chơi xử lý một cảnh ngắn). Áp dụng: mỗi câu mồi là một tình huống công việc nhỏ có hai hướng đều hợp lý, người chơi gõ một hai câu "mình sẽ làm gì, vì sao". Giữ tổng số câu phải gõ dưới 10.
2. **Kết quả là lời gợi ý, người chơi sửa được** (Morrowind: 10 câu rồi "bạn luôn có thể bỏ qua gợi ý"; Fallout 3 và New Vegas: đổi lại được ngay sau bài test; Pokémon Mystery Dungeon bản sau cho từ chối kết quả). Áp dụng: đoạn tự mô tả do AI viết hiện kèm nút "Chưa đúng, sửa lại", và ghi rõ đây là bản đọc tạm. Khớp D-02.
3. **Chỉ số lớn lên từ việc làm, không từ lời khai** (Persona 5: năm chỉ số, mỗi chỉ số năm bậc, tăng theo hoạt động hằng ngày và mở khoá quan hệ; Oblivion: nhân vật đề xuất lớp dựa trên cách bạn chơi màn dạo đầu). Áp dụng: biểu đồ sau Get to know me chỉ vẽ nét mờ "tự nhận"; nét đậm xuất hiện dần từ tín hiệu của 97 sự kiện nhập vai. Khớp D-03.
4. **Cho chơi trước, hỏi sau, và mời lại nhiều lần thay vì chặn một lần** (Duolingo: dời bước đăng ký ra sau bài học đầu, DAU tăng khoảng 20%; ba "tường mềm" bỏ qua được). Áp dụng: Get to know me xuất hiện sau nhiệm vụ đầu tiên, bỏ qua được, và được mời lại ở hai ba mốc sau. Khớp D-29.
5. **AI hỏi tiếp để xin một chi tiết cụ thể, không viết hộ; và nhắc lại đúng điều người chơi đã nói** (Hinge Prompt Feedback: "no auto-rewrites", bản thử đầu bị lỗi cứ đòi "viết thêm"; Until Dawn: nỗi sợ người chơi khai ra quay lại thành đạo cụ trong cảnh sau). Áp dụng: tối đa một câu hỏi tiếp cho mỗi câu trả lời, chỉ khi câu trả lời thiếu chi tiết; một nhiệm vụ về sau trích lại một ý người chơi đã viết. Trang hồ sơ trình bày theo kiểu Spotify Wrapped: mỗi thẻ một sự thật rút từ hành vi chơi.

### B. Ba phương án hoàng đạo, từ an toàn nhất tới táo bạo nhất

**Phương án 1 · Chòm sao trên "hộ chiếu" (thuần trang trí).** Mượn từ Animal Crossing.
- *Buff là gì:* một dòng chòm sao trên hồ sơ, một huy hiệu và màu viền, kèm đoạn mô tả **thiên văn và thần thoại** của chòm sao (sao sáng nhất, mùa nhìn thấy, tích truyện). Không có câu nào tả tính cách.
- *Chạm tới chấm điểm hay mở khoá:* không.
- *Người không tin:* mặc định tắt. Câu hỏi là "Bật lớp chòm sao cho vui không?", không hỏi và không lưu "bạn có tin chiêm tinh không". Tắt bất cứ lúc nào, tắt là mất hẳn khỏi giao diện.
- *Nói với cô:* đây là lớp trang trí theo chủ đề vũ trụ, nội dung là thiên văn và văn hoá; sản phẩm không khẳng định chòm sao nói gì về con người. Dẫn Carlson (1985) để cho thấy nhóm biết rõ chiêm tinh không dự đoán được tính cách.

**Phương án 2 · Mùa chòm sao và "sao dẫn đường" tự chọn (đối xứng, đổi được).** Mượn lịch phát đồ theo tháng của Animal Crossing và đá Standing Stone đổi được của Skyrim.
- *Buff là gì:* (a) mỗi tháng là mùa của một chòm sao, **mọi người chơi** nhận cùng một món trang trí và một thẻ truyện phụ của mùa đó; (b) người chơi tự chọn một "sao dẫn đường" bất kỳ, đổi lúc nào cũng được, ngày sinh chỉ dùng để gợi ý lựa chọn ban đầu. Sao dẫn đường gắn với một mục tiêu tự đặt cho tuần ("tuần này mình thử hỏi lại trước khi làm") và phần thưởng là bụi sao dùng cho đồ trang trí.
- *Chạm tới chấm điểm hay mở khoá:* không. Bụi sao là đơn vị riêng, không quy đổi sang điểm năng lực hay cấp nghề. Mười hai sao có giá trị ngang nhau.
- *Người không tin:* không bật thì vẫn nhận đồ theo mùa như một sự kiện lịch bình thường; phần "sao dẫn đường" là tuỳ chọn.
- *Nói với cô:* lợi ích không phụ thuộc ngày sinh, ai cũng nhận như nhau, nên không có bất công; cơ chế thật sự là tự đặt mục tiêu, chòm sao chỉ là cái tên. Dẫn bài học Oblivion → Skyrim: lợi thế cố định từ đầu bị người chơi xếp hạng và phải bỏ.

**Phương án 3 · "Phép thử Barnum" như một bài học (táo bạo, phải làm kỹ).**
- *Buff là gì:* người chơi bật lớp chòm sao được đọc một đoạn "mô tả theo cung" và chấm mức đúng; ngay sau đó game cho biết mọi cung đều nhận cùng một đoạn (thí nghiệm Forer), rồi đặt cạnh đó phần hồ sơ dựng từ **chính hành vi chơi** của họ. Phần thưởng là huy hiệu "Nhà hoài nghi" và một món trang trí.
- *Chạm tới chấm điểm hay mở khoá:* không.
- *Người không tin:* không bật thì không gặp. Người bật được báo trước là có một bất ngờ, và có phần giải thích sau đó; không dùng cho người chơi chưa qua bước đồng ý của người giám hộ.
- *Nói với cô:* đây là phần minh hoạ tương tác cho lập luận §1.1 của báo cáo về nhãn loại, dùng Forer (1949) và Carlson (1985). Rủi ro cần nêu thẳng: có yếu tố "đánh lừa có giải thích", nên cần lời báo trước và nên thử với vài người dùng trước khi đưa vào.

**Luật chung cho cả ba phương án:**
- Không có câu nào gắn cung với tính cách, điểm mạnh yếu hay nghề (Lu và cộng sự 2020: định kiến theo cung dẫn tới phân biệt đối xử dù cung không dự đoán được gì).
- Không đưa cung của người chơi vào ngữ cảnh của model khi AI viết phản hồi nhiệm vụ hay đoạn tự mô tả.
- Không có lời "vận hôm nay" tốt hay xấu (Clobert và cộng sự 2016: lời tử vi tích cực hay tiêu cực làm đổi kết quả làm bài).
- Chỉ hiện nội dung chòm sao **sau** khi người chơi đã xong Get to know me, để lời tự mô tả không bị cung "mớm".
- Nếu muốn có nét Việt: dùng con giáp của **năm hiện tại** làm chủ đề theo mùa cho mọi người; không gán con giáp theo năm sinh, vì cả lớp cùng tuổi sẽ cùng một con và con giáp gắn với niềm tin hợp, xung trong gia đình.
