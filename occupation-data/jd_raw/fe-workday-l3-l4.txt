About The Role

As a Front-End Engineer on the Workday Engineering team, you will be a core contributor to the Paradox browser extension project, building the front-end experiences that bring product's capabilities directly into users' browsers. This role is essential to ensuring our browser extension delivers a seamless, responsive, and accessible experience across browsers and devices.

Responsibilities:

Implementing clean, reusable front-end components based on UI/UX designs that are intuitive and visually polished
Ensuring responsive, accessible, and high-performance experiences across browsers and devices
Developing, maintaining, and improving Front-end components and integrations with third-party services and SDKs
Developing, testing, and maintaining browser extensions that extend product capabilities, ensuring cross-browser compatibility and security compliance
Partnering with engineering in Vietnam and product teams in the U.S. to refine requirements and plan technical tasks
Proposing solutions, estimating effort, and taking ownership of features end-to-end from design through deployment
Participating in code reviews, sharing best practices, and continuously improving code quality, user experience, and performance
Integrating backend and AI-related APIs into intuitive, reliable front-end experiences
Collaborating with backend and AI engineers to refine contracts, handle edge cases, and ensure smooth end-to-end flows
Recommending UX and workflow improvements based on user feedback and data to help shape innovative features
Other duties which are assigned by the Employer or authorized person of the Employer.

Our Approach to Flexible Work

With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote. Our approach enables our teams to deepen connections, maintain a strong community, and do their best work. We know that flexibility can take shape in many ways, so rather than a number of required days in-office each week, we simply spend at least half (50%) of our time each quarter in the office or in the field with our customers, prospects, and partners (depending on role). This means you'll have the freedom to create a flexible schedule that caters to your business, team, and personal needs, while being intentional to make the most of time spent together. Those in our remote "home office" roles also have the opportunity to come together in our offices for important moments that matter.
