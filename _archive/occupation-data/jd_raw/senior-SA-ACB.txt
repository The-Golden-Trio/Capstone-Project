Senior Solution Architect 
About the job
Key Responsibilities1. Architecture Review & Governance
Review architecture, solution design, and technology deliverables developed by internal teams and external partners.
Coordinate review activities with business, technology, and governance stakeholders.
Ensure identified issues, risks, and improvement actions are addressed prior to approval.
Monitor deliverable quality, completeness, and compliance with established standards and requirements.
2. Architecture & Solution Assessment
Assess architecture and solution designs to ensure alignment with enterprise principles, target-state architecture, and industry best practices.
Identify architectural risks, gaps, dependencies, and implementation challenges.
Evaluate design decisions from both business and technology perspectives.
Provide recommendations to enhance solution quality, scalability, maintainability, and operational effectiveness.
3. Solution Validation & Advisory
Facilitate architecture reviews, design walkthroughs, and technical discussions.
Evaluate the feasibility and practicality of proposed solutions, operating models, and implementation approaches.
Validate assumptions, recommendations, and design decisions proposed by project teams and technology partners.
Provide independent assessments and advisory support for architecture and technology-related decisions.
4. Quality Assurance & Acceptance Support
Validate deliverables against agreed quality standards, requirements, and acceptance criteria.
Ensure supporting documentation, knowledge transfer materials, and ownership arrangements are complete and adequately maintained.
Provide recommendations on deliverable readiness and approval status.
Support continuous improvement of architecture governance and review practices.

Qualifications & Experience
8+ years of experience in Enterprise Architecture, Solution Architecture, Digital Transformation, or related areas.
Experience in Banking & Financial Services, or large-scale transformation programs.
Experience working with technology vendors, system integrators, and cross-functional stakeholders.
Technical Expertise
Strong knowledge of TOGAF, BIAN, DDD, API, Integration, Cloud, and Microservices Architecture.
Ability to assess enterprise-scale architecture deliverables from both strategic and implementation perspectives.
Skills & Competencies
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills.
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
Experience facilitating architecture reviews and governance forums.
Ability to constructively challenge and validate recommendations from partners and vendors.
