# Shops and purchasable buffs in real games

> Researched 3 Nov 2026 for `research/reports/Kinh tế và sức khoẻ JobQuest.md`.
> WebFetch was blocked on Fandom, Stardew Valley Wiki, IGN, Prima: many figures come from search-result summaries of those wikis or fan guides and are marked **(snippet)**. Guides disagree on several values; treat every price as approximate.

## Summary

Real games sell buffs in three shapes: (1) short same-day or next-action boosts priced like a day's wage and gated by scarce time; (2) permanent upgrades bought with a separate meta-currency, not the main money; (3) cheap flat items that raise a stat or recover condition in small steps. Persona 5's Chihaya is the closest model: a reading costs about one shift of part-time work, lasts the rest of that day, is only offered some nights, and the relationship is opened by a one-off ¥100,000 payment refunded at rank 9. Almost every game caps stacking (one buff slot, escalating prices, limited time slots), so a rich player cannot buy past the intended pace. **Purchased skill buffs speed progress toward a fixed ceiling; they do not raise it.**

## 1. Persona 3/4/5

**Chihaya Mifune (Fortune), P5/Royal**

- Money Reading: doubles money from battles ended by All-out Attacks that day; ~¥5,000.
- Affinity Reading (rank 5 Royal, rank 7 original): deepens a chosen Confidant bond; ¥5,000.
- Fate / Special readings ¥10,000–20,000; names and rank-8 price differ across sources.
- Only at night on Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun; not on rainy days. [samurai-gamers](https://samurai-gamers.com/persona-5/fortune-confidant-chihaya-mifune/)
- Gate: ¥100,000 Holy Stone starts the relationship, returned at rank 9. [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/5472-persona-5-chihaya-mifune-fortune-confidant-choices-relationship-guide)

## 2. The Sims 4

- Reward Store traits bought with **Satisfaction Points (SP)**, earned from Wants and aspirations, not salary: Savant 4,000 SP (all skills faster), Morning Sim / Night Owl / Speed Reader / Mentor 1,000; Carefree 3,000, Brave 8,000; mood potions 200 SP each; Moodlet Solver 500. [Sims Community](https://simscommunity.info/2022/05/23/guide-to-satisfaction-points-rewards-in-the-sims-4/); [reward traits](https://simscommunity.info/2026/07/07/the-sims-4-reward-traits-guide/)
- Focused speeds mental and tech skills, Inspired creative skills; no confirmed percentage. [magicgameworld](https://www.magicgameworld.com/the-sims-4-moods-that-affect-skills/)
- Career pay is large and varies (Doctor §280/h, Stylist §500/h). [Sims Community](https://simscommunity.info/2023/01/13/highest-paid-careers-in-the-sims-4/) The big skill buffs use SP, so a high salary cannot buy a faster learning rate (interpretation).

## 4. Cross-cutting patterns

- **(a) Faster toward the cap vs higher cap.** Almost all bought skill buffs speed progress and leave the ceiling fixed (Luck Reading, Savant, mood potions, Persona's fixed rank thresholds). Hades raises the ceiling, but with a separate meta-currency.
- **(b) Temporary vs permanent.** Temporary: same day (Chihaya), until sleep (Stardew). Permanent: Sims traits, Mirror of Night, paid in a second currency.
- **(c) Next action vs duration.** Persona rest-of-day, Stardew real-time minutes, Sims moodlet timers, Slay the Spire single-use potions. A "next mission" buff fits JobQuest's 10–15 minute unit.
- **(d) Stopping the rich player.** One buff slot (Stardew); escalating prices (Slay the Spire removal, Persona tiered prices); weekday and time windows (Chihaya); stat-rank gates (Big Bang Burger, jobs); time-slot scarcity (Persona, FE3H). No documented numeric diminishing-returns formula found.
- **(e) Low-income phases.** Cheap meaningful items (¥500 coffee, 500-gold tea, 200 SP potion, 300g coffee) and free stat sources (Leblanc coffee, curry). Early money buys access more than raw power.
- **(f) Gifts and unlocks.** Money-gated: Holy Stone, tea leaves, expensive gifts. Time/affinity-gated: confidant ranks, keepsakes, rank-gated readings. Real games mix both: money opens the door, time deepens the bond.

## Not verified

Sims "Marathon Mind" and "Genius Brain" not found in reward lists (do not cite); Luck Reading exact price and rank-8 reading vary; Sims object-quality skill numbers; Stardew price scaling; Princess Maker 2 lesson costs; original Tokimeki jobs; no GDC or design-article source on buff caps.
