# Overtime, health, realistic job previews and salary realism

> Researched 3 Nov 2026 for `research/reports/Kinh tế và sức khoẻ JobQuest.md`.
> Marks: **[verified]** = abstract or official page read; **[secondary]** = summary or legal-advice site quoting the source. Labour Code articles 98, 105, 107, 108 were checked on the official signed scan (see §3).

## Summary

Realistic job preview (RJP) meta-analyses show honest previews help, with small effects, and the mechanism is perceived honesty, not lowered expectations. WHO/ILO tie 55+ h/week to stroke and heart-disease deaths, and long hours add little output. Vietnamese law caps OT at 40 h/month and 200 h/year (300 h in listed sectors), paid at 150/200/300%. No game-based RJP study and no Vietnamese salary-misperception study was found.

## 1. Realistic job previews

- **Earnest, Allen & Landis 2011**, *Personnel Psychology* 64(4), 52 studies, ~17,000 people: the main route to lower voluntary turnover is perceived organisational honesty, not lowered expectations. [verified, Warwick abstract](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/57084/)
- **Phillips 1998**, *AMJ* 41(6), 40 studies: lower recruitment attrition, lower initial expectations, higher performance, lower turnover; timing and medium moderate. [verified, abstract](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/effects-of-realistic-job-previews-on-multiple-organizational-outc/)
- Effect sizes small: corrected r ≈ −.06 voluntary turnover (Phillips), ≈ −.07 (Earnest et al.). [secondary]
- **Premack & Wanous 1985**, *J Appl Psych* 70, 21 experiments: lower initial expectations, more self-selection, commitment, satisfaction, survival. [secondary](https://qic-wd.org/node/437)
- **Games or simulations as RJP:** no peer-reviewed study with career outcomes; only a 2024 VR preprint (23 participants) and commercial items. JobQuest should claim "informed by RJP theory", not "proven".
- Including negatives helps mainly because it signals honesty.

## 2. Long hours and health

- **Pega et al. 2021**, *Environment International* 154:106595 (2016 data): 745,194 deaths from ischemic heart disease and stroke; 488 million people (8.9%) work 55+ h/week; attributable fractions 3.7% IHD, 6.9% stroke; reference 35–40 h. [verified](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34011457/)
- **WHO/ILO, 17 May 2021**: deaths up 29% since 2000; 72% men; Western Pacific and South-East Asia most affected; at 55+ h stroke +35%, IHD +17%; recommends capping hours and banning mandatory OT. [verified](https://www.who.int/news/item/17-05-2021-long-working-hours-increasing-deaths-from-heart-disease-and-stroke-who-ilo)
- **Kivimäki et al. 2015**, *Lancet* 386: IHD RR 1.13; stroke RR 1.33; stroke dose-response 1.10 (41–48 h), 1.27 (49–54 h), 1.33 (55+ h). Observational. [secondary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26298822)
- **Virtanen et al. 2018**, *Scand J Work Environ Health* 44(3), depressive symptoms: pooled RR 1.14; Asia 1.50, Europe 1.11, North America 0.97. [secondary](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10043538/)
- **Pencavel 2014/2015**: output proportional to hours below a threshold, rising at a decreasing rate above it [verified abstract](https://docs.iza.org/dp8129.pdf). Threshold ~49 h, 70 h ≈ 56 h output [secondary]. WWI munition workers: old, narrow sample.
- Sleep: thinly sourced (one Whitehall II thesis).

## 3. Vietnam

**Labour Code 45/2019/QH14** (passed 20 Nov 2019, in force 1 Jan 2021). **[verified]** Articles 98, 105 and 107 read on the signed scan from the Government portal, pages 37, 39 and 40: [vanban.chinhphu.vn record](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?pageid=27160&docid=198540), [signed PDF](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2019/12/45.signed.pdf). The PDF has no text layer; quotes below are transcribed from the page image.
- **Điều 105:** "Thời giờ làm việc bình thường không quá 08 giờ trong 01 ngày và không quá 48 giờ trong 01 tuần." "Nhà nước khuyến khích người sử dụng lao động thực hiện tuần làm việc 40 giờ đối với người lao động."
- **Điều 107 khoản 2:** OT needs the worker's consent (a); "không quá 50% số giờ làm việc bình thường trong 01 ngày; trường hợp áp dụng quy định thời giờ làm việc bình thường theo tuần thì tổng số giờ làm việc bình thường và số giờ làm thêm không quá 12 giờ trong 01 ngày; không quá 40 giờ trong 01 tháng" (b); "không quá 200 giờ trong 01 năm, trừ trường hợp quy định tại khoản 3" (c).
- **Điều 107 khoản 3:** up to 300 h/year for (a) export production of textiles, garments, leather, footwear, electrical and electronic goods, agro-forestry, salt and aquatic processing; (b) electricity, telecoms, oil refining, water supply and drainage; (c) work needing highly skilled labour the market cannot supply in time; (d) urgent work that cannot be postponed (seasonal, materials, weather, disaster, fire, power or material shortage, production-line failure); (đ) other cases set by the Government. Khoản 4: the employer must notify the provincial labour authority in writing.
- **Điều 108:** no cap in special cases (defence, security, disaster, etc.); the worker may not refuse.
- **Điều 98 khoản 1:** OT pay "ít nhất bằng 150%" on normal days, "ít nhất bằng 200%" on weekly rest days, "ít nhất bằng 300% chưa kể tiền lương ngày lễ, tết, ngày nghỉ có hưởng lương" for workers paid daily. Khoản 2: night work +30%; khoản 3: night OT a further +20%.
- 2025–26: a draft amendment is in appraisal; on 16 Sep 2026 the Ministry of Home Affairs said mandatory cuts to 40–44 h/week "chưa phù hợp". No OT-cap change found. [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/bo-noi-vu-neu-ly-do-chua-sua-luat-de-bat-buoc-giam-gio-lam-khu-vuc-tu-xuong-40-44-gio-tuan-100260916111851906.htm)

**Hours and income:**
- NSO 2024 (via Vietcetera): ~46% work 40–48 h, ~29% over 48 h; ILO average 45.3 h (2024). No sector table found. [Vietcetera](https://vietcetera.com/en/vietnam-was-once-among-the-worlds-top-20-for-working-hours-could-that-change)
- NSO Q3/2026 press release, read directly: average income 9.2 million VND/month; services 10.9, industry and construction 9.9, agriculture 5.4; men 10.3, women 7.9; urban 10.9, rural 8.0; income includes OT pay. [NSO](https://www.nso.gov.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Thong-cao-bao-chi-ve-tinh-hinh-lao-dong-viec-lam-QIII-nam-2026.pdf)
- Better Work Vietnam, garment (against the old 30 h/month cap): 8th synthesis (2014–15, 193 factories) almost 9 in 10 non-compliant on OT; excessive OT fell from 90% to 50% of factories by year 5. Dated. [8th synthesis](https://betterwork.org/?p=8546); [ILO review](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/ilo-ifc-programme-garment-industry-makes-workers%E2%80%99-lives-better-and)
- Burnout: Anphabe ~31% (year and method unverified); Gen Z 48% cite work-life balance as a reason to leave, 17% pay (Việc Làm 24h & Cốc Cốc, Dec 2025). Treat as context.

## 4. Salary realism for young people

- **Jensen 2010**, *QJE* 125: Dominican Republic, 2,250 boys; information on returns to schooling raised completion by ~0.2 years. [J-PAL](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/fr/node/1727)
- **Wiswall & Zafar 2015**, *RES* 82(2): students update earnings beliefs only partly; tastes dominate major choice. [secondary](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr500.html)
- **Hastings, Neilson & Zimmerman** (NBER w21300, Chile): earnings disclosure cut demand for low-return degrees by 4.6%. [NBER](https://www.nber.org/papers/w21300)
- Vietnam: no experimental study found.

## Implications for JobQuest

1. Frame OT, strain and low pay as ordinary job facts with real numbers, not warnings; honesty is what drives the benefit.
2. OT strictly opt-in, visible price; diminishing returns (Pencavel shape).
3. Anchor OT to the legal ladder (40 h/month, 200/300 h/year); show OT pay multipliers where the job pays OT; show "beyond the legal cap" as a distinct pressure event.
4. Health loss by hour bands (35–40, 41–48, 49–54, 55+), slow-onset, with a short evidence panel citing WHO/ILO.
5. Exposure varies by job and company type.
6. Show salary as a range with median, and note that published income includes OT.
7. A short debrief after the mission ("you worked X OT hours…") rather than a lecture before.
8. Claim "informed by RJP research", not "proven to improve career choice".
9. Use NSO tables directly with the data year; treat Anphabe and Better Work as dated.
10. Note that legal caps are as of Nov 2026 and may change.
